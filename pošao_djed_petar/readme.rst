################
Pošao djed Petar
################

*English translation "Grandfather Petar Set Off".*

:Written by: Grigor Vitez
:Date: circa 1957

Grandfather Petar sets out to stop the wind with everything he has; the wind wins.

Notes
=====

One of Vitez's best-loved short poems, for the youngest: a humorous picture of an old
man taking on the wind with all he has.

*Kapom*, *štapom*, *kaputom* and *prutom* are all "with" a cap, stick, coat and switch
(a thin stick); Croatian does it with the ending alone, so "with" is added in English.
*Al se vjetar ne da* is "but the wind doesn't let itself", won't give in.

The poem rhymes in pairs (*Petar* / *vjetar*, *kapom* / *štapom*, *kaputom* / *prutom*,
*da* / *djeda*); the rhymes are not kept.

Text from Lukin portal za djecu (pjesmicezadjecu.com, "Bakama i djedovima").
It is in Vitez's *Sto vukova* (1957), found by a search of that book's text, and is
named in a list of his poems in a Belgrade edition (*Bibliografija Jugoslavije*, 1964);
the date is that of *Sto vukova*.
