#######
Lullaby
#######

| Nina-nana, nina-nana,
| the snowy branch bends,
| under it a little bunny sleeps,
| his head sprinkled with snow.
|
| He dreams of the sun and butterflies,
| a green pasture and shepherds.
| Nina-nana, nina-nana,
| our little son one day
|
| will play with a butterfly,
| will stroke it ever so gently,
| so as not to break its delicate wing...
| Sleep, sleep, dear child.
|
| Nina-nana, nina-nana,
| beneath the white snowy branches
| a little bunny sleeps in the copse.
| Fine snow falls over him
| while my little son in the dusk
| lies on a soft feather bed
| and in his warm cradle
| all night dreams of powdery snow...
| Nina-nana, nina-nana...
