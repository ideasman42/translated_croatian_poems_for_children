#########################
Fetch Me the Moon, Father
#########################

| – Fetch me, Father, the moon,
| So that it shines a little beside me!
| Fetch me, Father, the moon,
| So that I may touch it with my hand!
|
| – The moon must shine up above,
| It must not be taken down.
| It must be with the stars,
| Lighting the way for the stars,
| So the little stars know the way home,
| When they have played their fill across the sky.
| And on the earth it must shine,
| So the hare can see to eat his supper,
| So the hedgehog can see to travel,
| So the mouse can see to scamper about,
| So the birds can sleep,
| And so that for you, my child,
| The moon may weave a gentle sleep
| From silver threads
| So that you may dream too.
