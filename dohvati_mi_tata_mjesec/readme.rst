########################
Dohvati mi, tata, mjesec
########################

*English translation "Fetch Me the Moon, Father".*

:Written by: Grigor Vitez
:Date: 1959

A child asks Father to fetch down the moon; Father explains everyone who needs it up there - the child included.


Translation
===========

- ``translated_en_gb_literal.rst`` keeps as close to the Croatian as English allows
  for reading against the original.

It keeps line numbers matching the original.


Notes
=====

A dialogue: the child's lines, then Father's answer, each opened with a dash as in
the source.

Father's reply is one long list of *da* clauses (so that...), each a creature that needs
the moonlight. The translation keeps them as a run of "so" lines.

*Zec* is both hare and rabbit; "hare" is the wild animal of the fields.

*San* is both sleep and dream. The moon weaves *san* so that the child *sniva* (dreams):
"sleep", then "dream".

Text from *Grigor Vitez: Izabrane pjesme* (lektire.skole.hr).
