###########
The Swallow
###########

| – Swallow, where have you been?
| – In the faraway world.
| – And what have you brought us?
| – Spring on my tail.
| – And how did you travel?
| – In the cabin of an aeroplane.
| – How much did the journey cost you?
| – About two or three million.
| – Where will you live now?
| – Here under the roof.
| – Is it in that old flat?
| – No, in a new one.
