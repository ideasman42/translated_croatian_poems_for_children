#########
Lastavica
#########

*English translation "The Swallow".*

:Written by: Grigor Vitez

A child asks the returning swallow where she has been and how she travelled, and she answers like a modern traveller.


Translation
===========

- ``translated_en_gb_literal.rst`` keeps as close to the Croatian as English allows
  for reading against the original.

It keeps line numbers matching the original.


Notes
=====

Swallows come back to Croatia in spring and nest under the eaves; Vitez has this one
answer like a grown-up home from abroad, by plane, with a new flat. *Proljeće na
repu* (spring on my tail): the swallow is the sign of spring.

*Stan* is a flat; the nest under the roof is her flat. *Miliona* is the older
form of *milijuna* (millions), of money.

The question lines rhyme with the answers (*svijetu* / *repu*, *aviona* /
*miliona*); the rhymes are not kept.

Text from *Grigor Vitez: Izabrane pjesme* (lektire.skole.hr), the edition the book
uses for Vitez, stanza breaks checked against the PDF page.

Mirko Kuten's *Hrvatski dječji pisci* (1991) has the same text. Lukin portal za
djecu (pjesmicezadjecu.com) prints a different version ("Lastavice, lastavice /
Gdje si bila?", "A koliko te let koštao?", "Zar u onom starom stanu?").
No date of first printing was found.
