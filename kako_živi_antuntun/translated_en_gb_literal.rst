##################
How Antuntun Lives
##################

| In the tenth village
| Lives Antuntun.
| He has a slightly
| Unusual mind.
|
| Every job he does
| In his own way:
| Eggs for hatching
| He plants in the garden.
|
| When it gets very dark,
| He scoops up the dark with a pot.
| A smashed egg
| He sews up with thread.
|
| Whether the food is salty,
| He listens with his ear.
| And a fish to sing
| He tries to teach.
|
| To the meadow he drives
| His bicycle to graze.
| To catch mice for him,
| He shuts in the pig.
|
| He feeds the geese on hay,
| Salts the sheep with snow.
| And he sets a broody hen
| To hatch him money.
|
| When he goes through the wheat
| He sits in a boat.
| Entirely in his own way
| Lives Antuntun.
