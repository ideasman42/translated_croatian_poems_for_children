##################
Kako živi Antuntun
##################

*English translation "How Antuntun Lives".*

:Written by: Grigor Vitez
:Date: 1956

Antuntun does everything his own way: he plants eggs, scoops up the dark with a pot and teaches fish to sing.

Notes
=====

Grigor Vitez's best-known poem: a run of the wrong tool for every job, each one
perfectly logical to Antuntun.

*U desetom selu* (in the tenth village) is the Croatian "far, far away" -
somewhere a long way off, past nine other villages.

*Antuntun* is the name *Antun* with its last syllable doubled, as a child might say it.

*Nasaditi kvočku* is to set a broody hen on eggs to hatch them; Antuntun sets her
to hatch money. *Čun* is a small boat or canoe.

The poem rhymes its 2nd and 4th lines (*Antuntun* / *um*, *radi* / *sadi*);
the rhymes are not kept.

Text from *Grigor Vitez: Izabrane pjesme* (lektire.skole.hr); picture-book
editions set it two lines to a page.
It prints "živi" in line 2 in lower case, corrected to "Živi" as in every other line
and the Zagreb Faculty of Teacher Education (likovna-kultura.ufzg.unizg.hr).
