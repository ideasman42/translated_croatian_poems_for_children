###########################
Svim na zemlji mir, veselje
###########################

*English translation "To All on Earth Peace, Joy".*

:Written by: Unknown
:Date: circa 1827

A carol on the angels' message: peace on earth, and praise for the Son God has sent.

Notes
=====

A traditional carol, built on the angels' song at the birth of Christ: "on earth
peace, good will toward men". Out of copyright.

*Budi* is an old jussive, "be it"; *polag* is "according to". *Sinka* is the fond,
small form of *sin* (son), kept as "little Son". *Posl'o* is the spoken form of
*poslao* (sent). *Diči* is "glorifies", from *dika* (pride, glory). *S Ocem, Duhom* is
"with the Father, (with) the Spirit"; the English supplies "and" so that the Spirit is
not read as another name for the Son.

The poem rhymes in couplets (*veselje* / *volje*, *duša* / *kuša*); the rhymes are
not kept.

Text as printed by the parish of Sveti Križ (zupa-svkriz.hr), punctuation
normalised. The date is the earliest printing found, in *Bogoljubne pisme* (1827);
Marijan Jaić gives its tune in his *Napivi* (1850). The old printings differ in small
words: "da bud' polak Božje volje" and "da je poslo" (1827), "Sada nebo navješćuje"
(1861), "Ar je poslo" (Antun Luci, 1876).
