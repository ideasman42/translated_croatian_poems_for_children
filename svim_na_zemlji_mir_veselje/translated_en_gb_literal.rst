##########################
To All on Earth Peace, Joy
##########################

| To all on earth peace, joy,
| be it according to God's will.
| This now heaven announces
| and a voice from heaven confirms.
|
| Every soul of good will
| let it taste sin no more,
| but praise, glorify God,
| that he has sent his little Son.
|
| His little Son, my God,
| equal with the Father and the Spirit,
| by the Holy Spirit conceived,
| of the Virgin born.
