#############
En, ten, tini
#############

*English translation "En, Ten, Tini".*

:Written by: Unknown
:Date: circa 1896

A counting-out rhyme made entirely of nonsense sounds.

Notes
=====

A traditional counting-out rhyme (*brojalica*): the children stand in a ring, one
points to each in turn on every beat, and the one pointed to on the last word is out,
or is "it". Out of copyright.

**The rhyme is untranslatable: every word is nonsense**, so the English is the Croatian.
"Sava" is not the river, nor "raka" a crab; "tika, taka" sounds like tick-tock.
Compare "eeny, meeny, miny, mo".

The one pointed to on "truf" is out.

Text from Hrvatska matica iseljenika's Croatian-learning site (ucimo-hrvatski.com),
matching a rhyme collected in Varaždin in 2007; the Institute of Ethnology and
Folklore Research holds a recording from Dobri Zdenci, 1971.
Many versions stop at "bija, baja, buf!"; others carry on with rude lines about a
granny baking bread.
