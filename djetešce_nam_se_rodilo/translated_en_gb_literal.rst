#############################
A Little Child Was Born to Us
#############################

| A little child was born to us,
| in a manger was laid,
| Jesus most dear, my God,
| I give you my heart, that I may be yours.
|
| Son of God the Father, and God himself,
| from heaven to earth came down to us,
| Jesus most dear, my God,
| I give you my heart, that I may be yours.
|
| The pure Virgin bore him,
| and with her own milk suckled him,
| Jesus most dear, my God,
| I give you my heart, that I may be yours.
|
| Jesus was the name the Father gave him,
| to redeem us, he sent him,
| Jesus most dear, my God,
| I give you my heart, that I may be yours.
