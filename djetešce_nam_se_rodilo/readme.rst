######################
Djetešce nam se rodilo
######################

*English translation "A Little Child Was Born to Us".*

:Written by: Unknown
:Date: circa 1864

A short traditional carol, each verse ending with the singer giving his heart to
Jesus.

Notes
=====

A traditional carol. Out of copyright.

*Djetešce* is the fond, small form of *dijete* (child). *Mileni* is "dearest",
"most beloved". *Bog sam* in line 5 is "God himself", not "I am God".
*Isus mu ime Otac da* puts the name first: the Father named him Jesus. That *da*
is the old past tense (aorist) of *dati*, "gave", like *siđe* and *posla*; the *da*
opening the next line is the conjunction "that". *Da sam tvoj* is a purpose clause,
"so that I may be yours".

The rhymes come in couplets (*rodilo* / *položilo*, *moj* / *tvoj*); they are not
kept.

**The text rests on web sources only:** hr.wikisource.org and Lukin portal za
djecu (pjesmicezadjecu.com) agree word for word, but no modern printed text was
found. Older printings differ: Šime Balenović's *Molitvenica i pjesmarica* (1864),
the earliest found and the source of the date, has "Isus srčeni" and "Sin Otca Boga
pravi sam", and a further verse beginning "Tovar i volak dvore mu" (the ass and the
ox serve him); a 1963 field recording of Croats in Hungary adds other verses again.
