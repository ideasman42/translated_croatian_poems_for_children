####################
Kako se pjesma pravi
####################

*English translation "How a Poem Is Made".*

:Written by: Zvonimir Balog
:Date: 1970

Anyone can make a poem: a row of laughter, a row of mountains, a row of seas, and a golden dove from the heart.

Notes
=====

The second poem of Balog's *Nevidljiva Iva* (Mladost, 1970), a recipe for a poem.

*Red* is a row, and also a line of verse: the poem is built a row at a time.
*Duga* is a rainbow. *Dvije – tri* (two – three) is the idiom for "two or three".
*Izvući* is to pull out, so the poet *draws out* the dove rather than sketching it.
*Gukati* is to coo, as a dove does.
Dalibor, Dodo and Vlatko are boys' names, there for the rhyme on *svatko*
(anyone); *i ... i ... i* (both ... and ... and) is given as "... too".

The poem rhymes in couplets (*svatko* / *Vlatko*, *gora* / *mora*); the rhymes are
not kept.

Text from Lukin portal za djecu (pjesmicezadjecu.com, "Stihovi za 10"), matching
the 1978 Mladost edition (a scan, read by OCR), which has "Dvije-tri duge".
