##################
How a Poem Is Made
##################

| Anyone can make a poem
| Dalibor and Dodo and Vlatko too,
| And here is how it is made:
|
| First a row of laughter is put in,
| then a row of high blue mountains,
| then a row of deep mysterious seas.
| One flower! Two or three rainbows
| One star! A little sadness.
| Then from his heart the poet draws out
| a golden dove to coo in the poem!
