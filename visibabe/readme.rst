########
Visibabe
########

*English translation "Snowdrops".*

:Written by: Ivo Kozarčanin

The first snowdrops ring like little bells by the brook, calling the spring.

Notes
=====

Snowdrops are the first flowers of the year, hanging their white heads like bells.
*Visibaba* is literally "hanging granny".

*Roniti* is to dive; the brook runs low through the field. *Graba* is a ditch.
*Izmeđ* is *između* (among), shortened. *Sirotani* are orphans, poor little ones,
alone in the cold. *Proljeće se kreće* is "spring is setting off".

The poem rhymes in couplets (*zvoni* / *roni*, *grabe* / *visibabe*); the rhymes are
not kept.

Ivo Kozarčanin (1911-1941) wrote it before his early death; no date of first
printing was found. Text from Lukin portal za djecu (pjesmicezadjecu.com), matching
Kozarčanin's *Pjesme, novele* (1975) and a quotation in Krklec's *Majmun i naočari*
volume of 1977 as far as their snippets show. Those print "izmed", likely "izmeđ"
lost in scanning; a 1967 music-school article has "iznad" (above).
