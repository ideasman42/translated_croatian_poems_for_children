#########
Snowdrops
#########

| What bell is that ringing
| by the brook that dives through the field?
| What white heads are those
| blooming among the withered grass?
|
| Those, this morning beside the ditch,
| are the first snowdrops to have sprung up,
| and now they ring in the early day
| like little orphans,
| so that the dewy flowers may bloom
| for spring is already on its way.
