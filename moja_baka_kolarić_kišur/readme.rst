#########
Moja baka
#########

*English translation "My Grandmother".*

:Written by: Zlata Kolarić-Kišur

A picture of grandmother: grey hair, spectacles, a smile and a gentle look.


Translation
===========

- ``translated_en_gb_literal.rst`` keeps as close to the Croatian as English allows
  for reading against the original.

It keeps line numbers matching the original.


Notes
=====

*Naočari* is an older word for spectacles (now *naočale*). *Sunca zraka* is "a ray
of the sun", the words in poetic order. *Eto* is "there", pointing.

The poem rhymes in couplets (*kosa* / *nosa*, *drag* / *blag*, *zraka* / *baka*);
the rhymes are not kept.

Text from Lukin portal za djecu (pjesmicezadjecu.com, "Bakama i djedovima"),
the only source found, which prints it again in its "Stihovi za 10".
No date of first printing was found.
