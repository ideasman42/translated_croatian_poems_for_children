########
Mi djeca
########

*English translation "We Children".*

:Written by: Grigor Vitez

The children will build a city of green trees, blue sky and joy, that no enemy can take, because it is in their hearts.

Notes
=====

A free-verse poem, often recited by children at school shows: *Suvremena metodika
nastave* (1985) notes it had been heard "countless times" at performances.

*Grad* is a town or city. *Krošnja* is the crown of a tree. *Najveći* is "biggest"
in size; "greatest" would suggest "best", which the Croatian does not say.

Text from *Grigor Vitez: Izabrane pjesme* (lektire.skole.hr), the edition the book
uses for Vitez, stanza breaks checked against the PDF page.

Quoted the same, in its ekavian form, in *Život* (1974). No date of first printing
was found.
