###################
The Birds' Songbook
###################

| This morning a book fell from the roof,
| The letters spilled out of it,
| Birds came, big and small
| And pecked their fill of letters.
|
| And now every day they spell out
| The various letters they swallowed.
| They shout out loud, and warble
| And chirp and tweedle
| And gurgle and cheep
| And chitter and peep,
| They squeak, twitter and trill,
| They screech, chirr and chirrup.
|
| They shriek, croak and coo
| And pipe and squeal,
| They babble, they chatter,
| They tootle, they tweet,
| They shrill, they whine,
| They carol, they twiddle,
| They lilt, they croon,
| They whistle and flute
| And they sing and they sigh...
|
| Every day the sun questions them,
| Reads to them from the golden book,
| And from dawn they learn, spell out
| Two or three letters to warble.
