################
Čemu služi djed?
################

*English translation "What Is a Grandfather For?".*

:Written by: Vera Zemunić

A grandfather tells stories, thinks and looks for his grandson, and plays horse, car, Batman and submarine.

Notes
=====

Like "Čemu služe mame" and "Čemu služe tate" in this book, which are by Nada Landeka,
it answers the question in its title; this pair is by Vera Zemunić.

The poem begins as the answer to its title: "(he is for this), so that..." *Da* is
"so that" in the opening stanzas; in the list after *radom:* it is "to" (to be a
horse). *Te skače* ("and leaps") is indicative, a break in that list, kept.

*Djeda* is the spoken form of *djed* (grandfather). *Iskazati se* is to prove oneself.
*Hrzati* is to neigh; *kreveljiti se* to pull faces. *Unučić* is the fond form of
*unuk* (grandson).

The rhymes (*pripovijeda* / *gleda*, *gradom* / *radom*, *hrže* / *brže*) are not
kept.

Text from Lukin portal za djecu (pjesmicezadjecu.com, "Bakama i djedovima"),
the only source found. No date of first printing was found.
