##########################
What Is a Grandfather For?
##########################

| So that to his grandson, a little boy,
| he tells stories.
|
| So that instead of him
| he both thinks and looks
| while they walk through town.
|
| But grandfather proves himself most
| by his work:
|
| to be a horse
| and to neigh,
| a car that races faster and faster,
| a supersonic aeroplane,
| Batman, and leaps from roof to roof,
| to stand on his head in the armchair,
| to be a clown and pull silly faces.
| A submarine in the bath
| and many more clever
| things to do.
|
| That is what grandfather is for.
|
| And he exists so that
| he keeps his little grandson company all day.
