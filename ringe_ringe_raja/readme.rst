Ringe, ringe, raja
##################

*English translation "Ringe, Ringe, Raja".*

:Written by: Unknown
:Date: circa 1899

The ring game: the children dance in a circle and crouch down on the last word.

Notes
=====

A traditional rhyme, out of copyright.

The children hold hands and walk round in a ring; on *čuč* they all crouch down,
as in "Ring a ring o' roses".

"Ringe, ringe, raja" is nonsense, from German *Ringel, Ringel, Reihe* (ring, ring,
row), kept as it is. *Muć* is from *mućkati* (to whisk, shake an egg), *čuč* from
*čučnuti* (to crouch).

Text from Lukin portal za djecu (pjesmicezadjecu.com). The date is from *Vienac*
(1899), which quotes "ringe, ringe raja" as a children's game. Other versions have
"zdjela puna jaja", or (in Serbia) "doš'o čika Paja".
