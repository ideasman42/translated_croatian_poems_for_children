#######
Da i ne
#######

*English translation "Yes and No".*

:Written by: Grigor Vitez

A girl would gladly say yes to the sun and to her family, and would say no to war, as all children would.


Translation
===========

- ``translated_en_gb_literal.rst`` keeps as close to the Croatian as English allows
  for reading against the original.

It keeps line numbers matching the original.


Notes
=====

The speaker is a girl: *rekla* and *htjela* are feminine forms, which English does
not show.

*Voliti* is both "to like" and "to love", so line 2 is "like" and line 5 "love".
*Nedin* and *Branin* are "Neda's" and "Brana's", two friends.
*Sve* in line 12 is "everything". In line 13 *što i* is "the same as", and the verb
left out after it is the conditional *bi rekla*, so the English keeps "would" rather
than adding a present "say".

The rhymes (*sja* / *DA* / *dva*, *rata* / *tata*, *sve* / *NE*) are not kept.

Text from *Grigor Vitez: Izabrane pjesme* (lektire.skole.hr), the edition the book
uses for Vitez, stanza breaks checked against the PDF page.

Mirko Kuten's *Hrvatski dječji pisci* (1991) has "Lako bih mu rekla DA" without
the colon, and "I Nedin tata i Branin tata". No date of first printing was found.
