##########
Yes and No
##########

| If anyone asked me:
| Do you like it that the sun shines?
| I would easily tell him: YES.
|
| If anyone asked me:
| Do you love Father and Mother
| And your two brothers?
| I would easily tell him: YES.
|
| If anyone asked me:
| Would you want there to be war
| And for your father to go to war
| And Neda's father, Brana's father
| And for everything to be black and sad?
| I would say the same as all the world's children:
| NO.
