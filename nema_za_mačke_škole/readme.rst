###################
Nema za mačke škole
###################

*English translation "There Is No School for Cats".*

:Written by: Grigor Vitez

The cat wants to go to school with Mira, but everything a cat needs, cats learn at home.


Translation
===========

- ``translated_en_gb_literal.rst`` keeps as close to the Croatian as English allows
  for reading against the original.

It keeps line numbers matching the original.


Notes
=====

*Torba* is a school bag, *đak* a pupil,
and *đačka* the pupil's: Mira says it proudly, letter-spaced in the print
("đ a č k a"), set in italics here.

*Upita Miru mačka* is "asked Mira the cat": the order leaves it open, and the cat
is the one asking. *Nema škole* is "there is no school", singular, kept in the
title. *Verati se* is to climb, as a cat climbs trees. Commas are added after
"can't" and "all that", where English needs them to read as the Croatian does.

The rhymes (*mačka* / *đačka*, *mole* / *škole*) are not kept.

Text from *Grigor Vitez: Izabrane pjesme* (lektire.skole.hr), matching Božo Ćorić's *Srpskohrvatski jezik za
strance* (1985). Lukin portal za djecu (pjesmicezadjecu.com) has "Kuda ćeš s
torbom?", lower-case line starts, and a stanza break before "Mački je dosta".
