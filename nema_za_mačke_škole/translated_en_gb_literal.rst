###########################
There Is No School for Cats
###########################

| – Where are you off to with the bag? –
| The cat asked Mira.
|
| – To school! I'm a pupil!
| This is a *pupil's* bag!
|
| – I'd go with you too! –
| The cat's eyes plead.
|
| – You can't, dear,
| There's no school for cats.
| It's enough for a cat
| To know how to purr,
| To know how to hunt,
| To know how to eat,
| To know how to climb
| And to miaow,
| And all that, cats
| Learn at home.
