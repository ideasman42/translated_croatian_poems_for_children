#######
Puž muž
#######

*English translation "Snail, Mister".*

:Written by: Unknown
:Date: circa 1896

Children tell a snail to put its horns out, or they'll sell its house.

Notes
=====

A traditional rhyme, out of copyright.

Said to a snail drawn into its shell, to make it put out its horns (eye stalks).

*Muž* is a husband or man, here only for the rhyme with *puž* (snail): "mister".
*Kazati* is "to show", in its older sense. *Smazati* is "to gobble up".

The rhymes (*van* / *prodam* / *duhan*, *kazati* / *smazati*) are not kept.

Text from Lukin portal za djecu (pjesmicezadjecu.com), with its capital "Ako".
The date is from *Zbornik za narodni život i običaje južnih slavena* (1896):
"Puž, muž, hodi van ... Dá ti baba na duvan!"; "Puž, muž, kaži roge van" is in
Klarić (1912), as cited by Biljana Schmid-Sikimić (1996).
