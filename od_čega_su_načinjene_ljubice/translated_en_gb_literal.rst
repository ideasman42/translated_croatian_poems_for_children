What Are Violets Made Of
########################

| What are violets made of
| That stand like white and blue
| little butterflies or little bugs
| In the grass?
| The white ones could have taken their colour from the snow
| Of which this winter there was lots and lots.
| The blue ones could have chosen their colour
| When the sun was setting.
| That may all be.
| That may all be.
| But what could the petals have been made of
| Under a bush in the quiet?
| And wherever did they find such a fine scent?
