*English translation "What Are Violets Made Of".*

:Written by: Grigor Vitez
:Date: 1959

A child wonders where the spring violets got their colours, and where they found their scent.

Notes
=====

Violets are among the first flowers of spring in Croatia, found under hedges
while the snow has hardly gone.

*Zimus* is "this last winter"; the doubled *mnogo mnogo* ("much much") is given as
"lots and lots". *Leptirići* and *bubice* are the fond, small forms
of *leptir* (butterfly) and *buba* (bug).

From *Kad bi drveće hodalo* (Mladost, 1959), and *Republika* the same year.
Text from Vitez's own *Kao lišće i trava* (1960), matching *Republika* (1959) and
Mirko Kuten's *Hrvatski dječji pisci* (1991). The printed copies run lines 2-3
as one long line ("...bijeli i plavi leptirići ili bubice"), so "leptirići" has a
lower-case letter; it is split here at "plavi", which rhymes with "travi",
as on Lukin portal za djecu (pjesmicezadjecu.com), so the lines stay level with
the translation. *Republika* has commas after "izabrati" and the first
"To sve može biti".
