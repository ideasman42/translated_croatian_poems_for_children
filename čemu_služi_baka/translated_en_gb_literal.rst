##########################
What Is a Grandmother For?
##########################

| Serving as a grandmother
| every one
| minds and looks after from morning till dark
| a little boy.
|
| For the little scamp she puts shoes on,
| takes shoes off,
| takes the little cardigan off,
| puts the little cardigan on.
| She warms the milk,
| and then she cools it.
|
| And all such things
| from morning till dark
| every
| grandmother
| does.
|
| And what for you
| does the grandson do, grandmother?
|
| He hugs me and says:
| - I love you, grandmother dear, very much!
