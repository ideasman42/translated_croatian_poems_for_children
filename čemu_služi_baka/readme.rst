################
Čemu služi baka?
################

*English translation "What Is a Grandmother For?".*

:Written by: Vera Zemunić

A grandmother looks after her grandson all day; what does he do for her?

Notes
=====

Like "Čemu služe mame" and "Čemu služe tate" in this book, which are by Nada Landeka,
it answers the question in its title; this pair is by Vera Zemunić.

*Nestašak* is a little scamp. *Vestica* is a small *vesta*, a knitted cardigan or
waistcoat. *Bakice* is the fond form of *bako* (grandmother), as said to her; it is given as
"grandmother dear", so the two forms can be told apart.

The rhymes (*baka* / *svaka* / *mraka* / *dječaka*) are not kept.

Text from Lukin portal za djecu (pjesmicezadjecu.com, "Bakama i djedovima"), which
also prints it, without the author's name, in "Stihovi za 10". No date of first
printing was found.
