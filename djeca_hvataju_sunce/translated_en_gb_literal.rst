######################
Children Catch the Sun
######################

| Sun, sun,
| Come here a moment!
| Come into my mirror!
|
| Look, we have caught
| A little sun!
|
| Hop!
| Already it has dashed onto the wall,
| Over the roof of the house
| Already it skips,
| Then it flits
| Flits
| Like a little bird into the trees.
| Its feathers are
| Shining silk
| The little bird
| Has no wings,
| And yet it flies
|
| Flies
| Flies...
|
| Into the little room where shadow reigns,
| And into the kitchen where Mother is.
|
| Mother, Mother,
| Here, we make you a present of the sun!
