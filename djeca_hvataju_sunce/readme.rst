###################
Djeca hvataju sunce
###################

*English translation "Children Catch the Sun".*

:Written by: Grigor Vitez
:Date: circa 1961

Children catch the sun in a mirror and send it dancing over the walls, into the house, as a present for Mother.

Notes
=====

The game of catching the sun in a mirror and sending the bright spot over walls and
roofs. The spot becomes a bird without wings.

*Hodi* is "come", a regional word; *hodi malo* is "come here a moment". *Lijetati*
(*lijeće*) is to fly about, flit, as against *letjeti* (*leti*), to fly; the poem uses
both, and so does the translation. *Perje joj je* is "her feathers are", the bird
being feminine in Croatian. *Poklanjati* is to give as a present, not just to give,
hence "make you a present of".

The rhymes (*malo* / *ogledalo* / *zatrčalo*, *kuće* / *skakuće*) are not kept.

Text from Lukin portal za djecu (pjesmicezadjecu.com), with its stanza breaks,
including the one before "Leti / Leti..."; it is not in *Grigor Vitez: Izabrane
pjesme*. Stjepko Težak quotes the poem (1979, 2005). Dated from Vitez's
*Iza brda plava* (1961), which a book search matches for its title; not seen in print.
