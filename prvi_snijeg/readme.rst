###########
Prvi snijeg
###########

*English translation "The First Snow".*

:Written by: Gustav Krklec
:Date: circa 1960

The first snow turns everything white, town and village, river and hill.

Notes
=====

A poem of one colour: *bijel* (white) comes back in almost every line.
In Edo Vajnaht's grade-2 reader *Zlatna lađa* (Školska knjiga), and in many since.

*Djed Mraz* (Grandfather Frost) brought presents at New Year in former Yugoslavia.
*Blistati* is to glitter or sparkle, and *sjaj* a gleam or brilliance.
*Sipiti* is
for snow or fine rain to fall softly and thickly.

The poem rhymes *bijelo* / *selo* / *cijelo* and *gaj* / *sjaj*; the rhymes are not
kept.

Text from Krklec's *Majmun i naočari* (*Odabrana djela*, book 6, 1977); the stanza
breaks as on Lukin portal za djecu (pjesmicezadjecu.com). The date is the first
time it is found in print, quoted in *Savremena škola* (1960).
Zvonimir Balog's anthology (1975) and Lukin portal punctuate it
"Sve je bijelo, sve je bijelo:" with lower-case line starts, and end it
"tiho sipi, sipi snijeg".
