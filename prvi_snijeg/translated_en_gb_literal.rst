##############
The First Snow
##############

| All is white. All is white.
| Town and village, field, grove.
| The whole day, the whole evening
| Everywhere a white gleam glitters.
|
| White birch, white path.
| White star, white flower.
| The white beard of Grandfather Frost
| everywhere the flight of white snowflakes.
|
| All is white. All is white.
| White river. White hill.
| The whole day, the whole evening
| Quietly sifts down the first snow.
