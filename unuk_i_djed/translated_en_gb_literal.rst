########################
Grandson and Grandfather
########################

| So the grandson babbles to grandfather:
| – Oh, if only they'd take me to the Moon
| In a space rocket,
| You'd see how one flies,
| How one flies!
|
| And on the Moon, what's more –
| Now that's something for children!
| There the force of gravity is much, much less.
| Just the right place for contests!
| You can hardly even picture it.
| You can easily jump over a hill.
| And when you start to run,
| It's as if the wind carries you:
| In three steps you cover a kilometre!...
|
| While the grandson was thus weaving
| Adventures on the Moon,
| He slipped and fell
| Into a ditch full of water.
|
| And at that, grandfather to the grandson,
| Holding out his hand to him:
|
| – It's lovely, lovely to stroll through space
| And to reach all the hills of the Moon,
| But, you see, it can get in your way,
| If you don't know how to walk well on the ground.
