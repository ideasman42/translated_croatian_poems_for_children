######################
Pliva patka preko Save
######################

*English translation "A Duck Swims Across the Sava".*

:Written by: Unknown
:Date: circa 1964

A counting-out rhyme: a duck swims the river with a letter on its head, and the news is not good.

Notes
=====

A folk rhyme, sung or chanted while counting out in children's games;
the one pointed to on the last word is out. It is traditional and out of copyright.

The Sava is the river that runs through Zagreb.

The rhyme is a joke on a love letter, delivered by duck.

This single verse is the common form, as quoted in *Vijenac* 222 (Matica hrvatska, 2002),
with "navrh" written as one word. Some versions go on for more verses, which vary
from place to place (e.g. "Preplivala patka Savu, / smočila si nije glavu...").
