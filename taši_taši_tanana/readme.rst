##################
Taši, taši, tanana
##################

*English translation "Taši, Taši, Tanana".*

:Written by: Unknown
:Date: circa 1969

A clapping rhyme for a baby, with sugar in a silk scarf for supper.

===========

Notes
=====

A traditional rhyme, out of copyright.

Said while clapping the baby's hands together, like "pat-a-cake". "Taši, taši,
tanana" is nonsense, kept as it is.

*Marama* is a headscarf or kerchief, here used as a bundle.

The rhymes (*tanana* / *marama*, *šećera* / *večera*) are not kept.

Text from Lukin portal za djecu (pjesmicezadjecu.com); the space it has before the
last "!" is dropped. The date is from a Bosnian version, "Tašun, tašun tanana, /
I svilena marama", in *Građa* (Academy of Sciences of Bosnia and Herzegovina, 1969).
