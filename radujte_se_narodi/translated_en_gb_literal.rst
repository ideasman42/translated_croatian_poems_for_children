####################
Rejoice, You Nations
####################

| Rejoice, you nations, when you hear the news
| that Jesus was born in a blessed hour!
| Every nation hear, hear,
| and to Bethlehem draw near, draw near!
|
| See God's mystery in a tumbledown little stable
| and who suffers poverty on that hard little straw.
| That mystery hear, hear,
| and to the manger draw near, draw near!
|
| Mary rejoices with Joseph, watching,
| an angel sings "Gloria" flying through the air.
| And you, the whole world,
| greet the Saviour, greet!
