#################
Ide maca oko tebe
#################

*English translation "The Cat Goes Round You".*

:Written by: Unknown
:Date: circa 1967

A ring game: the cat walks round the circle and drops her tail behind one child, who must chase her.

Notes
=====

A traditional ring game, sung. The children crouch in a ring while the *maca* (cat)
walks round the outside carrying her "tail", a handkerchief or the like. At the end of
the song she drops it behind one child, who picks it up and runs after her, or the
other way round the ring, to win the place back. Out of copyright.

*Maca* is the fond word for a cat, "pussycat"; *Mijo* (short for *Mihovil*, Michael)
is a common name for a tomcat, here any child in the ring. The children who aren't
"blind" see the tail dropped behind them.

*Čuvaj rep* (guard your tail) is given as "mind your tail", the everyday English
warning, with *Mijo* moved to the end of the line as English puts a name.

Text from Naklada Bombon's game pages (nakladabombon.hr) and mojerazglednice.com,
which agree. A shorter version stops at line 4, as "da ne budeš slep" (so you won't
be blind), with the *ekavian* *slep* for *slijep*, rhyming with *rep*.
