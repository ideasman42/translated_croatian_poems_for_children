###############################
The Bunny and the Little Stream
###############################

| On one winter night
| there where a high hill stands
| the little stream froze
| and the snow covered it.
|
| And one little bunny
| looks for that stream everywhere,
| where it is, where it has vanished to
| that weighs on his breast.
|
| And he cries, cries, the young bunny,
| for that little stream
| grieves, grieves the bunny now
| grieves with all his heart.
|
| And sad, the bunny thinks:
| why, where is that stream?
| perhaps it follows the swallows
| to the far southern land.
