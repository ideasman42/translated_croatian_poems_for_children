##################
What Book for Whom
##################

| Imagine if everyone went to school –
| Both those that run on four legs,
| And those whom wings carry –
| What a strange school that would be!
|
| And what, then, would their books have to be like?
|
| Say, pussycat, would you need a book too?
| – Yes, for me and my grey colleague
| A book is needed where mice breed.
|
| And you, white pigeon?
| – For me, such a book where every letter
| Is a grain of wheat or a little poppy seed.
|
| And you, bear Furcoatson?
| – For me, a book where every line
| Is fragrant and sweet honey.
|
| And you, owl night-watcher?
| – Well, you know that in the dark, Auntie Owl reads,
| So – a black book and glowing letters.
|
| And you, crab backward-goer?
| – I need only books of our kind,
| Where the words and letters are backwards.
|
| And you, bunny Bunnyson?
| – My book, it is my long-held wish,
| Should have leaves of cabbage and kale.
|
| For such books, do not worry!
| You will get them all,
| As soon as on the oak
| The first carp begin to grow.
