########
Pred san
########

*English translation "Before Sleep".*

:Written by: Stanislav Femenić
:Date: circa 1961

Evening falls quietly, the moon forges its sickle without a sound, and sleep comes.

Notes
=====

A lullaby of one or two words to a line, to be read slowly. The new moon is a
*srp* (sickle), and the moon is forging it, silently, like a smith.

*Veče* is *večer* (evening). *Evo* is "here is", as one points. *Sasvim tiho* is
"completely quietly", given as "ever so quietly": "quite quietly" would read as
"fairly quietly".

The poem rhymes *Veče* / *Teče*, *kuje* / *čuje* and *Noći* / *Doći*; the rhymes are
not kept.

Text from Lukin portal za djecu (pjesmicezadjecu.com, "Uspavanke"), the only full
copy found. It is in Femenić's *Puž na ljetovanju* (Mladost; 2nd edition listed in
*Croatica*, 1973). The date is the first time the poem is found in print, in a
1961 bibliography of periodicals (*Bibliografija Jugoslavije*).
