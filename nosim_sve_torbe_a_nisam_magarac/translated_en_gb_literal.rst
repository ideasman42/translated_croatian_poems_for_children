##########################################
I Carry All the Bags but I Am Not a Donkey
##########################################

| I do not like to fight with anyone
| And I never will touch
| When they egg me on and say:
| You don't dare touch his ear.
|
| When the teacher lets us out of school
| First we all walk in a line,
| And afterwards, as soon as he cannot see us any more,
| They fight with the ones from the other village.
|
| I do not even like to talk about it.
| What I like best is when we go home
| And someone shouts: Who'll be the donkey?
| Then I say: Put the bags on me!
|
| And they all hang them on my shoulders.
| And it is not heavy for me, because I know I am carrying
| Jela's bag too. I could for her
| Carry, surely, thirty-three bags.
|
| But I would admit to no one that I carry them all
| Because of Jela!... Whoever perhaps thinks I am
| A real donkey, I know: he is mistaken, and does not know...
| A donkey, as everyone already knows, has long ears.
