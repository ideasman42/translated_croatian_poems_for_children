###############################
Nosim sve torbe a nisam magarac
###############################

*English translation "I Carry All the Bags but I Am Not a Donkey".*

:Written by: Dragutin Tadijanović
:Date: 1935

On the way home from school a boy offers to carry everyone's bags, for one reason he won't admit.

Notes
=====

Dated 1935 and first printed in *Hrvatska revija* (1937), in the cycle
"Dani djetinjstva" (Days of Childhood); in *Srebrne svirale* (Silver Pipes, 1960), the
poet's book of childhood poems, it is poem IX of that cycle. It is in free verse,
without rhyme, as the boy might tell it.

*Magarac* (donkey) is also the word for a fool or dunce; the boy plays the donkey
for the bags, but is no fool. The last line is his proof.

Line 2 has no object; "touch" means touch the other boy, as in line 4. *Ne smiješ*
is literally "you may not"; said as a dare it means "you don't dare". Daring a boy
to touch another's ear is a way of starting a fight.

*Jela* is a girl's name; *Jelinu torbu* is "Jela's bag". *Metnite na me* is
"put on me", *imade* an older form of *ima* (has).

Text from the poet's *Sabrane pjesme* (1975), with the same words in *Pjesme i proza*
(1969), *Gozba* (1990) and *Srebrne svirale*; the stanza breaks as on mojzagreb.info
and in other printings online. Dated in *Sabrane pjesme*
"Zagreb (Runjaninova ulica 4), 23. srpnja 1935." (23 July 1935).
The title is printed without a comma in the poet's own editions.
A school-reader version online has lower-case lines and small changes
("niti govoriti", "misle"); it is not followed.
