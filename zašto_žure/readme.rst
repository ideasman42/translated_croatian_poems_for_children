##########
Zašto žure
##########

*English translation "Why They Hurry".*

:Written by: Gustav Krklec
:Date: 1952

A hare sees motorcyclists racing by and can think of only one reason for it.

Notes
=====

From *Telegrafske basne* (Telegraph Fables, 1952): fables of four lines each, named
after the telegraph because Krklec wanted their messages short and quick.
The moral is left to the title and the last line, rather than spelled out.

Each of us judges the world by our own troubles: to a hare, anyone in a hurry must be
running from hunters.

*Zec* is a hare, *zeka* the fond word for one, as "bunny".

In line 2 *kako žure* follows the verb of seeing, so it is "as they hurried",
not an exclamation "how they hurry".

Text from Lukin portal za djecu (pjesmicezadjecu.com) and lektire.me, which agree;
set in Profil Klett's school reader (hr.izzi.digital).
