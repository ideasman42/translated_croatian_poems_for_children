##########################
Saint Nick Walks the World
##########################

| Saint Nick walks about the world,
| looking for his children,
| because for every good child
| he has some little thing.
|
| Tra-la-la-la, tra-la-la,
| Saint Nicholas will come!
|
| And in front of our house he will stop
| because we have been good,
| so to dear Saint Nick
| today we are very dear.
|
| He has read our wishes
| that fall from our souls,
| all our little shoes
| will be full of gifts.
|
| And where the children are not good,
| there the switch is taken up,
| behind him comes black Krampus,
| the switch is their pay.
