########################
Sveti Niko svijetom šeta
########################

*English translation "Saint Nick Walks the World".*

:Written by: Mira Preisler

Saint Nicholas goes from house to house with gifts for good children, and Krampus follows with a switch for the rest.

Notes
=====

The best-known Croatian song for Saint Nicholas' Day (6 December); words by Mira
Preisler, music by Lujza Kozinović, a nun. On the eve, children clean their shoes
or boots and set them in the window; good children find sweets and small presents
in them in the morning, others a *šiba* (switch, a bundle of birch twigs), brought by
*Krampus*, the devil who goes with the saint. Today the šiba is gilded and comes
with the sweets.

The refrain is sung after every verse, twice each time.

*Niko* and *Niki* are fond, short forms of *Nikola*. *Stvarcu koju* is "some little
thing". *Što se s duša rune* is "that fall from our souls", as grain falls from the
ear: the wishes come from the heart. *Laćati se šibe* is "to take up the switch".

Text from Lukin portal za djecu (pjesmicezadjecu.com) and the description of the
Mi Klinci recording (YouTube), which agree word for word; the authors as credited
by Menart's release. No printed text or date was found. Lukin portal has
"Tra-la-la-la-la-la-la" for the refrain; the Mi Klinci form is kept, as sung.
A reworked version (BupTiBu) has "sva pisma otvara" (he opens all the letters) in
the third verse.
