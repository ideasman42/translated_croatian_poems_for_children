###########
Grga Čvarak
###########

| If you asked anyone
| in my neighbourhood,
| in Zelengaj:
| — Who does everything the wrong way round
| who shoots at cats with a catapult,
| who jumps over the wall,
| who takes down swallows' nests,
| who pulls the hens by the tail,
| who's always fighting with the children,
| ready to flare up in a quarrel
| like a smouldering log?
|
| The answer will always be:
| — Grga Čvarak.
|
| But if you asked in my neighbourhood,
| in Zelengaj:
| just the same:
| — Who was up and about all night
| to give his sick mother
| aspirins, lemonades,
| and if you asked just the same
| who cried very, very hard
| because little Mila
| grazed her knee
| when she fell
| right into the ditch?
|
| The answer will always be:
| — Grga Čvarak.
|
| Why is he like that?
| Who would know?
| One moment he is good,
| the next he is bad,
| one moment he would give anyone his heart,
| the next he would fight a wolf to the death,
| skinny, thin as a dry twig
| — Grga Čvarak.
