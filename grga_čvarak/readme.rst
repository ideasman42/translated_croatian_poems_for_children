Notes
=====

From *Grga Čvarak* (Mladost, 1967), Zvrko's best-known book: poems about the boy
Grga Čvarak and the animals and people around him, long a grade-2 lektira
(required reading).

The title poem: the naughtiest boy in the neighbourhood, and the kindest.

*Čvarak* is a pork crackling, a fitting surname for a tough little boy.
*Zelengaj* (green grove) is a neighbourhood of Zagreb. A *ugarak* is a
half-burnt log that flares up again; *suharak* is something dried, here a dry twig.
*Skako* and *plako* are the spoken forms of *skakao* (jumped about, ran about) and
*plakao* (cried). *Klati se s vukom* is to fight a wolf to the death.

The poem rhymes in couplets (*naopačke* / *mačke*, *zida* / *skida*) and on
*-arak*; the rhymes are not kept.

Text from the 4th edition of *Grga Čvarak* (Mladost, 1974), a scan read by OCR,
matching Lukin portal za djecu (pjesmicezadjecu.com, "Stihovi za 10"), which has
"pračke" for "praćke", a comma after "naopačke", and hyphens for the dashes.
