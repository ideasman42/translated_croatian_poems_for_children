#################
Iš'o medo u dućan
#################

*English translation "Teddy Went to the Shop".*

:Written by: Unknown
:Date: circa 1968

A bear forgets his manners in the shop and is sent out; the next day he remembers.

Notes
=====

A traditional counting-out rhyme (*brojalica*): the children stand in a ring, one
points to each in turn on every beat, and the one pointed to on the last word is out,
or is "it". Out of copyright.

The one pointed to on *van* (out) is out. The second verse, the happy ending, is also
said on its own to small children, as a lesson in manners.

*Medo* is a pet name for a bear, as "teddy". *Iš'o*, *rek'o* and *doš'o* are the
spoken forms of *išao* (went), *rekao* (said) and *došao* (came).

Text from Lukin portal za djecu (pjesmicezadjecu.com), with "Ajde" as in Hrvatska
matica iseljenika's Croatian-learning site (ucimo-hrvatski.com); the Institute of
Ethnology and Folklore Research holds a recording from Dobri Zdenci, 1971.
Line 7 is also found as "Evo medi šećera" (here is sugar for the bear).
