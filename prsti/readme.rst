*English translation "Fingers".*

A finger rhyme for small children, counting from the thumb down to the little finger, which gets the tickle.

Notes
=====

A traditional rhyme, out of copyright.

Said holding a small child's hand, taking each finger in turn; on the last line the
little finger is wiggled and the child tickled. The finger names in brackets are
part of the source, and handy for learning them.

*Žganci* are a maize porridge, a plain country dish, kept as it is. *Figa* (a fig)
is the rude fist with the thumb between the fingers: "nothing for you", here for
the cat. "Cigu-ligu" is nonsense.

Text from Lukin portal za djecu (pjesmicezadjecu.com), with its semicolon after
the last "ovaj", likely a slip for a colon. The date is from a kajkavian version in
*Zbornik za narodni život i običaje južnih Slavena* (1911): "Ovaj ide v lov ...".
