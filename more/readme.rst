####
More
####

*English translation "The Sea".*

:Written by: Zlata Kolarić-Kišur

Even the sea must rest from the winds, and it breathes as it sleeps.

Notes
=====

A calm sea still rises and falls, as if breathing in its sleep.

*More* (sea) and *mora* (must) sound almost the same, a play English can't keep.
*Jednom* is "once", here "sometime". *I* (and, also) before *more* is "even".

The rhymes (*odmoriti* / *boriti*, *njiše* / *diše*) are not kept.

Text from Lukin portal za djecu (pjesmicezadjecu.com), the only source found,
with its capitals and its missing full stop after line 4. Not checked against print;
no date found.
