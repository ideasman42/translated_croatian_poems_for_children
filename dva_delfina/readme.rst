###########
Dva delfina
###########

*English translation "Two Dolphins".*

:Written by: Gustav Krklec
:Date: circa 1977

On the boat from Split to Hvar, a girl watches two dolphins playing in the waves.


Translation
===========

- ``translated_en_gb_literal.rst`` keeps as close to the Croatian as English allows
  for reading against the original.

It keeps line numbers matching the original.


Notes
=====

A summer crossing on the Adriatic, from Split to the island of Hvar, past Brač.

*Orati* and *plužiti* both mean to plough; the ship's wake is the furrow.
*Lađa* is a ship or boat. *Čas ... časkom* is "one moment ... the next".
*Javiti se* is to show oneself, make oneself known. *Vale* are waves.
*Igra* is play or a game; *ploviti* is to sail, here of dolphins, to swim.

The poem is in rhymed couplets (*more* / *ore*, *Hvara* / *stvara*); the rhymes are
not kept.

Text from Lukin portal za djecu (pjesmicezadjecu.com), except line 8, where it has
"motre"; Krklec's *Majmun i naočari* (*Odabrana djela*, 1977) has *motri*
(watches), agreeing with Katarina. The dash in line 11 is the website's hyphen.
Dated from the 1977 book, the earliest printing found; Mladost also published it
as a picture book with Boris Dogan in 1980.
