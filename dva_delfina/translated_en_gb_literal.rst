############
Two Dolphins
############

| A white ship, a deep-blue sea.
| Through the sea the ship ploughs a furrow.
|
| The ship ploughs a furrow through the sea,
| and over the furrow a gull circles.
|
| All the way from Split to Hvar
| the white ship makes a furrow.
|
| And from the ship Katarina
| watches the play of two dolphins.
|
| Now they are up, the next moment down,
| playful, in good spirits.
|
| Now they are down, the next moment up –
| the sea is their swing.
|
| They swing, swim, leap
| then vanish towards Brač.
|
| The child watches the blue waves.
| The dolphins show themselves again.
|
| She sees their tails and fins.
| Their play goes on for ever.
|
| Now far off, now they are nearer,
| until the ship reaches Hvar.
