##############
Udala se jesen
##############

*English translation "Autumn Got Married".*

:Written by: Zvonimir Balog

Summer marries autumn, and autumn weeps for joy - in chestnuts.

Notes
=====

The whole poem hangs on one coined verb, *kestenilo*, made from *kesten* (chestnut):
autumn's tears of joy are the chestnuts dropping from the trees for days.
English can coin the same verb, so "it chestnutted" is kept rather than explained away.

The title uses *udati se*, said of a bride; in the poem summer *oženilo* autumn,
said of a groom. Autumn is the bride.

This is the whole poem. Text from the Kaštela town library (gkk.hr), matching DVD Sesvete
(dugoselo.dvds.hr).
