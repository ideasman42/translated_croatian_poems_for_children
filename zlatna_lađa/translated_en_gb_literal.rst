###############
The Golden Ship
###############

| If to me, young as I am,
| A ship were given,
| With a prow of silver
| And with a stern of gold,
|
| From many-coloured wood
| I would hew oars.
| From white silk
| I would weave her sails.
|
| I would sail her
| Across the deep-blue wave;
| Sailing, rowing
| From shore to shore.
|
| And when I had gone
| Round the whole world,
| That ship of mine
| I would fill with everything.
|
| I would bring my mother
| Treasure aplenty of every kind,
| My grandfather a pipe with a stem
| Of ivory.
|
| For my brothers a bow, arrows,
| And two golden swords;
| For my sister a doll and a basket
| Of sweet oranges.
|
| Then for a long time there would be
| Tales and talk
| About that glorious voyage
| Across the grey-blue sea.
