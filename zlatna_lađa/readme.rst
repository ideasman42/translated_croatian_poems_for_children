###########
Zlatna lađa
###########

*English translation "The Golden Ship".*

:Written by: Vladimir Nazor
:Date: 1929

A boy dreams of a ship of silver and gold, sailing round the world and coming home with gifts for everyone.


Translation
===========

- ``translated_en_gb_literal.rst`` keeps as close to the Croatian as English allows
  for reading against the original.

It keeps line numbers matching the original.


Notes
=====

Vladimir Nazor (1876-1949) is one of the founders of modern Croatian literature;
the poem is out of copyright.

The speaker is a boy: *mladom*, *tesao*, *tkao* are masculine.

*Lađa* is an older, poetic word for a ship or boat.

*Seka* is a fond word for a sister.

*Obašao* (went round) is an older form of *obišao*; *dosti* (plenty) of *dosta*.

*Modar* (deep blue) and *sinji* (grey-blue, the colour of the open sea) are the
traditional colours of the sea in Croatian folk poetry.

The text follows *Vladimir Nazor: Izabrane pjesme* (a scanned edition on pdfcoffee.com),
which starts every line with a capital.
Lukin portal za djecu (pjesmicezadjecu.com) and the sites copying it have a different
6th stanza - "Brajku mome, Vlajku, / divnoga konjica, / a sekici lutke / i zlatna kolica"
(for my brother Vlajko a splendid little horse, for my little sister dolls and a golden
cart) - possibly from a school reader. The edition's stanza is used here, as it also
appears in a separate school presentation.
