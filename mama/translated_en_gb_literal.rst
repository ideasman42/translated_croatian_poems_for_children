######
Mother
######

| Stars there are without number
| when darkness falls,
| the brightest of all
| is my mother!
|
| The cups of flowers are
| of a thousand colours,
| the loveliest of them
| is my mother!
|
| Neither white pearls,
| nor precious stones,
| can take the place of
| the little face dear to me!
|
| All the wonders of the earth,
| the beauties that are here,
| I would always give
| for my mother's kiss!
|
| The sun shines for us
| high up above,
| and beside me, happiness:
| my mother's eye!
