####
Mama
####

*English translation "Mother".*

:Written by: Mladen Pokić

Mother set against the stars, flowers and jewels of the world, and outshining them all.

Notes
=====

Lines 4 and 8 say the same thing with the words swapped - *moja je mama*, *mama je moja* -
to rhyme with *tama* and *boja*. English word order cannot be swapped the same way,
so both read "is my mother".

*Čaška* is a little cup, and the calyx of a flower; "cups of flowers" keeps the picture.

*Lišce* is the diminutive of *lice* (face): "little face".

"Uvijek bih dao" is masculine - the speaker is a boy. English does not show it.
Lukin portal za djecu prints "bi"; *bih* (I would) follows the IZZI school edition
(hr.izzi.digital) and standard Croatian.

*Pusa* is a child's word for a kiss.
