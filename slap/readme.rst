####
Slap
####

*English translation "The Waterfall".*

:Written by: Dobriša Cesarić
:Date: 1938

One small drop wonders what it means to a whole waterfall, and finds it helps make the rainbow.

Notes
=====

Written for adults, and the opening poem of Cesarić's *Spasena svjetla* (1938);
he named a later selection of his poems after it. Read in the upper grades of
Croatian schools, on how each small part counts.

*Duga* here is a rainbow, not the word for "long", spelled the same; *šara* is a
pattern, streak or colour, here the rainbow's colours.
*Mogo* is the spoken form of *mogao* (could), as Cesarić wrote it.

The poem is in rhymed couplets (*slap* / *kap*); the rhymes are not kept.

Text from Cesarić's *Slap: izabrane pjesme* (Matica hrvatska, 1970), with the same
words in many printed anthologies and essays. Some have a comma after "slap",
or "mogao".
