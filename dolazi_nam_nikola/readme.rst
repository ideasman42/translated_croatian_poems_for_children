#################
Dolazi nam Nikola
#################

*English translation "Nicholas Is Coming to Us".*

:Written by: Mladen Pokić

A clean little boot waits in the window for Saint Nicholas; in the morning, the present.

Notes
=====

On the eve of Saint Nicholas' Day (6 December) children clean their boots and set them
in the window; in the morning they find sweets and small presents in them.

*Starac sveti* (the holy old man) is the saint. *Čizmica* is the fond, small form of
*čizma* (boot); *sunašca* of *sunce* (sun). *Zjene* are the pupils of the eyes; the
translation says so in full, as "pupils" alone could be read as schoolchildren.

The poem rhymes in couplets (*blista* / *čista*, *stvar* / *dar*); the rhymes are not
kept.

Text from Lukin portal za djecu (pjesmicezadjecu.com), where line 9 is printed
"Ko´sunašca", with an accent for a space; *ko* is the spoken form of *kao* (like).
