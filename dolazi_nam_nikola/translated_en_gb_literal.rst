########################
Nicholas Is Coming to Us
########################

| By the window gleams
| A clean little boot,
| For the holy old man it waits,
| He is coming from afar!
|
| When in the early hours
| The blue morning dawns,
| The first thing will be -
| To look for the present!
|
| Like little suns
| The pupils of the eyes will shine,
| Holy old man, thank you,
| You think of me too!
