###################
Children in Traffic
###################

| In the town there is truly
| a real muddle,
| as if there were taking place
| some important race.
|
| Everyone in all directions
| tirelessly hurries,
| like aeroplanes
| they rush too fast.
|
| But all the same, everything is stopped
| by one proper traffic light!
|
| With its red light
| it will give a sign
| that everyone must
| stop at once.
|
| Respect all the signs
| within your range
| and especially look after
| children in traffic!
