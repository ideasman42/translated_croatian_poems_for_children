###############
Djeca u prometu
###############

*English translation "Children in Traffic".*

:Written by: Josipa Franjić Radulović
:Date: circa 2014

The rush of town traffic, stopped by one traffic light, and a reminder to look out for children.


Translation
===========

Notes
=====

A companion to "Semafor": both turn on the traffic light bringing order to the rush.

The last stanza speaks to the drivers, not the children.
*Poštujte* (respect!) is the imperative, matching *čuvajte* two lines below, as in the
narrated copy in Profil Klett's IZZI school edition (hr.izzi.digital). Other copies,
including Naklada BoMboN's, print "Poštujete" (you respect), most likely a slip.

*U svome dometu* is "within your range" - the signs you can see from where you are.

From the collection *Sreća* (Happiness), as published by Naklada BoMboN (nakladabombon.hr).
