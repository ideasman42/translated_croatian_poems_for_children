####
Paun
####

*English translation "The Peacock".*

:Written by: Grigor Vitez

His Majesty the Peacock announces that he sings beautifully, far better than the nightingale.

Notes
=====

The peacock is the finest-looking bird and has one of the ugliest voices, a harsh
scream; this one thinks otherwise. The nightingale, small and plain, is the finest
singer.

*Glas* is a voice; *glasić* a little one, *glasičić* a littler one, and the peacock
keeps adding the ending until the voice is all but gone. The English does the same
with "-let", one for each *-ić*. *Ptičić* is a little bird.

The poem is in rhymed couplets (*Prvi* / *krvi*, *punom* / *krunom*); the rhymes are
not kept.

Text from *Grigor Vitez: Izabrane pjesme* (lektire.skole.hr); no second copy or date was found. The last line
starts in lower case there, and is kept.
