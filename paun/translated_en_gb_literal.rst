###########
The Peacock
###########

| His Majesty Peacock the First,
| Descendant of peacock royal blood,
| With royal robes full of jewels
| And with a shining royal crown
| Said this morning loud and clear:
| – I sing wonderfully! I sing beautifully!
| The nightingale to me is just a little birdie,
| And his voice is a wretched voiceletlet,
| I tell you – a tiny voiceletletletlet!
