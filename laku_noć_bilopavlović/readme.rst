########
Laku noć
########

*English translation "Good Night".*

:Written by: Tito Bilopavlović

Good night to the letters, Father's pipe, the sandcastle, the alarm clock and everyone.


Translation
===========

- ``translated_en_gb_literal.rst`` keeps as close to the Croatian as English allows
  for reading against the original.

It keeps line numbers matching the original.


Notes
=====

A list of goodnights, as a child says them going to bed, to everything in the day.

*Velika slova* (big letters) are capitals. *Kula u pijesku* is a sandcastle.
*Jednoj maloj* is "to one little (girl)", the girl left unnamed. A *neboder*
("sky-tearer") is a tall block of flats.

The rhymes in the lines (*luli* / *kuli*, *budilici* / *ptici* / *slici*) are not
kept.

Text from Lukin portal za djecu (pjesmicezadjecu.com, "Stihovi za 10"), the only
source found. No date of first printing was found.
