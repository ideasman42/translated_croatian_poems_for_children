########
Moj zeko
########

*English translation "My Bunny".*

:Written by: Zlata Kolarić-Kišur

A child tells a bunny what's lovely about him, short tail and all.


Translation
===========

It keeps line numbers matching the original.


Notes
=====

For the youngest, in rhymed couplets.

*Zekan* is a fond, bigger form of *zeko* (bunny). *Brci* are whiskers, and a man's
moustache; *frkati brke* is to twirl a moustache, as a proud man does.
*Kusast* and *kusorep* are "stubby" and "bob-tailed".

The rhymes (*zekana* / *mekana*, *dugačko* / *junačko*) are not kept.

**Text from one source only**, Lukin portal za djecu (pjesmicezadjecu.com, "Stihovi
za 10"); no printed copy or date was found. It is used in teaching the youngest
readers (*Suvremena metodika nastave hrvatskoga jezika*, 1992).
Only the second line starts with a capital there, and is kept.
