#################
The Drawn Snowman
#################

| It is winter already. The cold shaves bitterly.
| In the mornings the frost patterns the window-panes.
| But still the snow is hiding somewhere,
| and without snow winter has no charm.
|
| And while winter in its pale garb
| treads the cold streets,
| for comfort, the pupils with white chalk
| draw a snowman on the blackboard.
