##################
Nacrtani snjegović
##################

*English translation "The Drawn Snowman".*

:Written by: Ratko Zvrko
:Date: circa 1967

Winter has come but the snow has not, so the pupils draw a snowman on the blackboard.


Translation
===========

- ``translated_en_gb_literal.rst`` keeps as close to the Croatian as English allows
  for reading against the original.

It keeps line numbers matching the original.


Notes
=====

"Studen ljuto brije" - the cold shaves bitterly - is the Croatian way of saying a wind
cuts like a razor. It is kept as the picture rather than smoothed to "bitter cold".

*Šarati* is to pattern or doodle: the frost draws on the windows just as the pupils
draw on the board.

*Ruho* is a robe or garment, an old and poetic word; "garb".

Text from *Grga Čvarak* (Mladost, 1974), where the last line begins with a capital,
"Snjegovića"; lower case is kept here, as the snowman is not a name.
