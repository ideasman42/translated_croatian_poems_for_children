###################
A Quiet, Quiet Song
###################

| Quietly the blackbird sleeps in the bush,
| And more quietly still the grass grows.
| Quietly the dark pine rustles,
| And more quietly still the cherry ripens.
| Quiet is the bunny beneath the branches,
| And more quietly still the plants dream.
| Dreams of many colours are dreamt by
| Flowers, forest, fields, ploughland.
| Everywhere dreams are scattered:
| The primrose dreams its yellow dream,
| Deep blue dreams the wood anemone,
| Violet – the violet,
| The blue sky – the forget-me-not,
| A white spark – the daisy,
| And deeply dreams the night,
| You deeper still... Good night!
