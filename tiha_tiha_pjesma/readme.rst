#################
Tiha, tiha pjesma
#################

*English translation "A Quiet, Quiet Song".*

:Written by: Grigor Vitez
:Date: circa 1961

Everything in nature falls asleep and dreams in its own colour; the night dreams deeply, and the child more deeply still.

Notes
=====

A goodnight poem: each line is quieter than the one before, and the flowers dream
in their own colours.

*Zoriti* is to ripen. *Njiva* is a ploughed field. The flowers are spring
flowers: *jaglac* (primrose), *šumarica* (wood anemone), *ljubičica* (violet), *potočnica* (forget-me-not, "brook
flower") and *tratinčica* (daisy). *Ljubičasto* (violet, the colour) and
*ljubičica* (the violet) are the same word in Croatian, as in English.

*Modro* is a deeper blue than *plavo*, so the anemone dreams "deep blue" and the
forget-me-not "the blue sky". *Nebo plavo* is *plavo nebo* inverted (as in *Iza
brda plava*): the sky itself, which the flower dreams, not the colour sky-blue.

The poem rhymes in couplets (*spava* / *trava*, *šumori* / *zori*); the rhymes are
not kept.

Text from *Grigor Vitez: Izabrane pjesme* (lektire.skole.hr), the edition the book already
uses for Vitez, page 14,
checked against the page, which prints it as one stanza. Lukin portal za djecu
breaks it into stanzas. The same words are quoted in *Naša škola* (1965),
which has "polje" for "polja". The date is that of *Iza brda plava* (1961), found
by a search of that book's text.
