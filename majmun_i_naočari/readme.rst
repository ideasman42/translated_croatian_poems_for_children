################
Majmun i naočari
################

*English translation "The Monkey and the Spectacles".*

:Written by: Gustav Krklec
:Date: 1952

An old monkey gets new glasses and boasts of what he can do with them.


Translation
===========

Notes
=====

From *Telegrafske basne* (Telegraph Fables, 1952): fables of four lines each, named
after the telegraph because Krklec wanted their messages short and quick.
The moral is left to the title and the last line, rather than spelled out.

The joke is in the pause before the last words: the new glasses are not for reading.
The dash before "buhe lovim" is kept for it.

*Naočari* is an older word for spectacles; standard Croatian now says *naočale*,
as in the title of Krklec's later collection, *Majmun i naočale* (1967).

Text from a transcription of *Majmun i naočale* (Mladost, 1967) in čovjek-časopis
(covjekcasopis.art.blog), and the school site lektirici.weebly.com.
