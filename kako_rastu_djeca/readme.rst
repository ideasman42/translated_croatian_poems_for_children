#################
Kako rastu djeca?
#################

*English translation "How Do Children Grow?".*

:Written by: Grigor Vitez
:Date: 1966

Questions and answers about children: how they grow, run, swim, chatter, play, wash and sleep.


Translation
===========

- ``translated_en_gb_literal.rst`` keeps as close to the Croatian as English allows
  for reading against the original.

It keeps line numbers matching the original.


Notes
=====

Each stanza asks a question, answers it with a run of comparisons, some lovely and
some funny (they swim like slippers), and closes on a rhyming line.

*Rasti iz nogavica* (to grow out of the trouser legs) is said in Croatian as in
English. *Kozlići* are young goats, kids; "little goats" is used because "kids" in
English reads as children. *Žagoriti* is the busy noise of many
voices, as of a classroom. A *hambar* is a granary or corn bin. *Kišu* is "they
sneeze", from *kišati*, a form of *kihati*. *Sjenice* are
tits, the small birds (great tits, blue tits).

Each stanza's last two lines rhyme (*trava* / *rukava*, *voda* / *hoda*); the rhymes
are not kept.

Text from *Grigor Vitez: Izabrane pjesme* (lektire.skole.hr), the edition the book
uses for Vitez.

The same text is in Krunoslav Kuten's *Hrvatski dječji pisci* (1991), in its
selection from *Igra se nastavlja* (1966), the source of the date; the
*Croatica* bibliography (1973) lists the poem in a later edition of that book.
