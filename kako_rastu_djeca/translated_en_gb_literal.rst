#####################
How Do Children Grow?
#####################

| How do children grow?
| Like willows out of water, like grass in the meadow,
| Children grow out of their trouser legs and sleeves.
|
| How do children run?
| Like little goats, like butterflies, like fast water,
| A child would rather run than walk.
|
| How do children swim?
| Like dolphins, like slippers, like boats,
| Depending on how each child manages.
|
| How do children chatter?
| Like jackdaws, like broken engines, like bees,
| Whether they are cross or whether they are glad.
|
| How do children play?
| Like children. Children play so many games
| That they would not fit even in five granaries.
|
| How do children wash?
| Like ducks, like eels, like cats when they sneeze.
| Bathed children smell of spring.
|
| How do children sleep?
| Like kittens in a basket, like young tits,
| And sweetly, sweetly, sweeter than chocolate.
