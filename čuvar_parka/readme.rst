###########
Čuvar parka
###########

*English translation "The Park Keeper".*

:Written by: Enes Kišević

A park keeper changes one word on his sign, and the park empties of everything he loves.


Translation
===========

- ``translated_en_gb_literal.rst`` keeps as close to the Croatian as English allows
  for reading against the original.

It keeps line numbers matching the original.


Notes
=====

The joke is one changed word: *travu* (grass) becomes *mrave* (ants), a near-rhyme.
The signs are kept in capitals as in the source, and translated word for word -
"keep off the grass" is the usual English sign, but it loses the parallel.

Watching the path for ants, the children walk with their heads bowed, as if in grief;
the rest of the poem follows them into mourning.

*Prhnuti* is the whirr of a bird taking off. Here it is the egg that does it,
rendered "took wing".

Text from Anto Zirdum's blog (antozirdum.wordpress.com).
