###############
The Park Keeper
###############

| A certain park keeper,
| out of revenge, pure and true,
| instead of DO NOT TREAD ON THE GRASS
| wrote: DO NOT TREAD ON THE ANTS!
|
| And all the children went
| along the path with heads bowed.
| The ant's tear ran
| down the face of the green grass.
|
| The birds flew away from the park,
| from the nest an egg took wing,
| gone was the anthill,
| gone was the children's clamour.
|
| And the leaves flew off the branches.
| The lawn bowed down in sorrow.
| On a bench the keeper dreams
| that he is racing over the grass with the children.
