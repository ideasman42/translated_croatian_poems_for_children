*English translation "Invisible Iva".*

Being invisible lets Iva do as she likes, until she has toothache and has to see the dentist.

Notes
=====

The title poem of *Nevidljiva Iva* (Mladost, 1970), Balog's best-known book, for
which he won the Grigor Vitez award; long a grade-4 lektira (required reading).
Nonsense with a dark joke: the dentist can't see Iva, so he works by ear.

*Brčine* is "big moustaches", the grown-ups. *U najboljem redu*, literally "in the
best order", is the idiom "in perfect order". *Ni dužnu ni krivu* is literally
"neither owing nor guilty", given as the English idiom "through no fault of her
own". *Oslonivši se o svojstva sluha* is "relying on the properties of hearing",
in a mock-learned tone.

The rhymes come at the ends of the short lines (*liznuti* / *kliznuti*,
*Iva* / *zbiva*, *sluha* / *uha*); they are not kept.

Text from the 1978 Mladost edition (p. 43), checked against the page image,
matching a typed copy of the Budućnost (Novi Sad) edition line for line.
That edition has "Pa joj je zubar" and closes the dash after "sluha";
the dash is left open here, as in the Mladost print.
