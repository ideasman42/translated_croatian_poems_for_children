#############
Invisible Iva
#############

| Every ice cream
| she could lick,
| out of school she could slip.
| Invisible Iva saw
| what in the most hidden
| places goes on;
| how people when they are alone
| do all sorts of things,
| even those who wear
| big moustaches.
| And Iva did not have to eat
| soup and mushroom stew,
| nor learn the alphabet.
| Everything was in perfect order
| until Iva's teeth began to ache,
| through no fault of her own.
| So the dentist
| — relying on the properties of hearing
| instead of a tooth pulled out
| both her ears,
| two locks of hair
| and her head itself from her shoulders.
| But Iva is still alive —
| right in my thoughts
| she dwells.
