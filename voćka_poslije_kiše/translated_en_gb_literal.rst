#############################
The Fruit Tree after the Rain
#############################

| Look at the little fruit tree after the rain:
| It is full of drops, and so it sways them.
| And there glitters, lit up by the sun,
| The wondrous splendour of its branches.
|
| But let the sun hide just a little,
| All that magic disappears.
| It is again, as at first,
| An ordinary, small, poor tree.
