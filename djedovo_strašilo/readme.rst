################
Djedovo strašilo
################

*English translation "Grandfather's Scarecrow".*

:Written by: Jadranka Čunčić-Bandov

Grandfather's scarecrow doesn't frighten the sparrows: they eat his lettuce.

===========

Notes
=====

*Strašilo* is from *strašiti* (to frighten), "a frightener". *Trta* is a homely word
for fright; *vrapce će biti trta* is "the sparrows will be in a fright".
*Kuda koji* is "each wherever he goes". *Iz čistog mira* (out of pure peace) is
"for no reason at all", out of the blue.

The rhymes (*vrta* / *trta*, *koji* / *stoji*, *smesti* / *jesti*, *mira* /
*šešira*) are not kept.

Text from Lukin portal za djecu (pjesmicezadjecu.com, "Stihovi za 10"),
the only source found. No date of first printing was found.
