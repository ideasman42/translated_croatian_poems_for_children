#######################
Grandfather's Scarecrow
#######################

| My grandfather made
| a scarecrow
| in the middle of the garden,
| thinking that
| the sparrows
| would get a fright
| and that they would flee,
| each his own way,
| when they saw the scarecrow
| standing there.
|
| But they did not
| let themselves be put off
| but instead
| began
| to eat his lettuce.
|
| Well, who is still afraid,
| for no reason at all,
| of an old coat and hat?
