###
Jež
###

*English translation "The Hedgehog".*

:Written by: Unknown

A warning rhyme: don't touch the hedgehog, or there'll be tears.

Notes
=====

A traditional rhyme, out of copyright.

*Boc* is the sound of a prick. *Iglice* are little needles, the hedgehog's spines.
*Bjež* is *bježi* (run away), cut short.

The rhymes (*iglicama* / *ručicama*, *jež* / *bjež*) are not kept.

Text from Lukin portal za djecu (pjesmicezadjecu.com), where it is printed twice,
as a counting rhyme and an autumn rhyme; no printed source or date was found.
Some kindergartens add an opening couplet about the hedgehog curled up asleep.
