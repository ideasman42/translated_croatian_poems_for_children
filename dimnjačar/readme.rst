Dimnjačar

*English translation "The Chimney Sweep".*

:Written by: Grigor Vitez

If I were a chimney sweep I could be as black as I liked, climb over the roofs, and give a sad child a bristle for luck.


Translation
===========

- ``translated_en_gb_literal.rst`` keeps as close to the Croatian as English allows
  for reading against the original.

It keeps line numbers matching the original.


Notes
=====

In Croatia, as across central Europe, a chimney sweep brings luck: people touch one,
or hold a button when one passes. Here the child would be free to be as black as he
likes, which no child is allowed.

*Smio bih* is "I would be allowed to" (*smjeti*, to be permitted), not "I could":
the point is that nobody would scold. *Nagaravljen* is "covered in soot".
*Nitko mi ne bi ništa* is "nobody would do anything to me". *Danomice* is
"day after day". *Iščupati* is "to pluck out"; the verb stays on its own line
so the lines of the last stanza match.

The 2nd and 4th lines of each stanza rhyme (*ognjišta* / *ništa*, *veću* / *sreću*);
the rhymes are not kept.

Text from *Grigor Vitez: Izabrane pjesme* (lektire.skole.hr), the edition the book
uses for Vitez, stanza breaks checked against the PDF page.
