#################
The Chimney Sweep
#################

| If I were a chimney sweep, I would be allowed to be black
| Like the blackest cauldron from the hearth,
| I would be allowed to walk down the street covered in soot
| And nobody would do a thing to me.
|
| I would carry brushes on plaited wires,
| One smaller and one bigger,
| On which every bristle
| Brings luck.
|
| I would go round the houses as if under a black mask,
| Only my white teeth and two eyes would laugh,
| I would climb above the roofs of the houses
| And look at the town from up high.
|
| And when I met some child who was sad
| And whom misfortune beats day after day,
| I would let him, from my brush,
| Pluck out one bristle.
