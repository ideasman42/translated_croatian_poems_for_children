#############
Mišji Kolumbo
#############

*English translation "Mouse Columbus".*

:Written by: Nada Iveljić
:Date: 1973

A little mouse sails with Columbus, comes home a hero, and in the end prefers his hole.

Notes
=====

A mock-heroic poem: fame, honours and a "contingent" of cheese, for a mouse who
would rather be at home.

*Želudac mu se za leđa prilijepio* (his stomach stuck to his back) is the Croatian
for being starving. *Kontingent* is a quota or consignment, an official word, there
for the rhyme on *kontinent*. *Reponja* is "one with a tail", a made-up name for a
mouse. *Bio je glavni* (he was the main one) is everyday Croatian for being the one
who counts, the top dog.

The rhymes (*otkrio* / *plovio*, *kontingent* / *kontinent*) are not kept.

First printed in the children's magazine *Radost* (1973, no. 5), as listed in
*Bibliografija Jugoslavije*. **Text from one source only**, Lukin portal za djecu
(pjesmicezadjecu.com, "Stihovi za 10").
