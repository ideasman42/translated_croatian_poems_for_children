##############
Mouse Columbus
##############

| When Columbus discovered America,
| on his ship
| a little mouse was sailing.
|
| With the sailors he went hungry, lived in dread.
| His stomach stuck to his back.
| He suffered a great deal.
|
| But in return, as soon as he came home,
| he was the main one,
| a little mouse famous above all.
|
| They gave him a great consignment of cheese,
| because he had discovered
| a new continent.
|
| He was entered in all the mouse encyclopaedias
| as a brave seafarer.
| They even put him on show for money.
|
| Mouse Columbus everyone called him.
| Later he went on three more voyages,
| then he grew tired of ships.
|
| Living in a hole seemed sweeter to him.
| Although he discovered America with Columbus,
| he was still only an ordinary long-tail.
