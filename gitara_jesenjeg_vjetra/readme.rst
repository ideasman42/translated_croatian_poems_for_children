######################
Gitara jesenjeg vjetra
######################

*English translation "The Autumn Wind's Guitar".*

:Written by: Grigor Vitez
:Date: 1964

The autumn wind takes up its old guitar and sings, and the leaves dance down to the ground.


Translation
===========

- ``translated_en_gb_literal.rst`` keeps as close to the Croatian as English allows
  for reading against the original.

It keeps line numbers matching the original.


Notes
=====

The poem is shaped like what it describes: the long lines of the wind's song, then the
short lines of the leaves, then single words falling one per line.
The translation keeps the layout line for line.

In print the falling lines are set as a picture: two columns of "list / po / list"
stepping across the page, then "pao" stepping down diagonally, like leaves drifting.
Here, with each line centred, they are given as plain lines.

Text from *Grigor Vitez: Izabrane pjesme* (lektire.skole.hr).

*Zapjevao* is to strike up a song, *pjevao* to sing: the wind starts, then keeps on.
