#######################
The Lark's Morning Song
#######################

| Up high!
| Up high I will rise!
| To the sun I will
| To wake it
| Go
| Go
| Go...
|
| I will take along
| A tamburica
| A tamburica
| A tamburica
|
| I will pour little bells down
| Over the green
| Yellow
| And blue
| Endless field.
|
| Where is my nest?
| Where are my chicks?
|
| I will have to
| Come down
| Come down
| Come down
| To look in on the chicks...
|
| Then once again I will
| To the sun
| Go
| Go
| Go...
