##############
Eci, peci, pec
##############

*English translation "Eci, Peci, Pec".*

:Written by: Unknown
:Date: circa 1967

Probably the best-known Croatian counting-out rhyme.

Notes
=====

A traditional counting-out rhyme (*brojalica*): the children stand in a ring, one
points to each in turn on every beat, and the one pointed to on the last word is out,
or is "it". Out of copyright.

"Eci, peci, pec" is nonsense, kept as it is. *Peci* happens to be "bake!" in Croatian,
but is not meant so here.

Text from Hrvatska matica iseljenika's Croatian-learning site (ucimo-hrvatski.com);
the same words on Lukin portal za djecu (pjesmicezadjecu.com), Bebimil and in a
rhyme collected in Varaždin in 2007. Serbian versions swap the roles:
"ja sam mali zec, / ti si mala prepelica" (a little quail).
