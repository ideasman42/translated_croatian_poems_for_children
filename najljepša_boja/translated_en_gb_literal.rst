#########################
The Most Beautiful Colour
#########################

| The most beautiful colour is green –
| – thinks the cow.
| Everything ought to be green,
| Then everything would be beautiful,
| Then everything would be beautiful
| like grass.
|
| Still more beautiful is white –
| – thinks the cat.
| The white colour of milk.
| If it were white, if it were white –
| How much more beautiful the river would be!
|
| All colours are beautiful:
| Red and yellow and blue and white –
| – thinks the bee,
| When it flies around the flowers.
| All colours are beautiful
| When they are flowers.
|
| This one isn't beautiful, nor this, nor this –
| – thinks the owl.
| Each of those colours is ugly, each, each, each,
| Only the colour of darkness is beautiful.
