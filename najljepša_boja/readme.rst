##############
Najljepša boja
##############

*English translation "The Most Beautiful Colour".*

:Written by: Grigor Vitez
:Date: circa 1960

The cow, the cat, the bee and the owl each know which colour is the most beautiful.


Translation
===========

- ``translated_en_gb_literal.rst`` keeps as close to the Croatian as English allows
  for reading against the original.

It keeps line numbers matching the original.


Notes
=====

Each animal's favourite colour is the colour of what it loves: grass, milk,
flowers, and for the owl, the night.

*Lijeće* is a poetic form of *leti* (flies).

The poem rhymes the animal with the line before it (*krava* / *trava*, *mačka*,
*pčela* / *bijela*, *sova* / *ova*); the rhymes are not kept.

Text from *Grigor Vitez: Izabrane pjesme* (lektire.skole.hr), matching Vitez's *Kao lišće i trava* (1960), the
earliest printing found, as far as the scan shows.
