##############
Nogomet u bari
##############

*English translation "Football in the Pond".*

:Written by: Ratko Zvrko
:Date: 1967

The frogs play the mosquitoes and midges at football, and the frogs' defender finds a simple way to stop a goal.

Notes
=====

From *Grga Čvarak* (Mladost, 1967), Zvrko's best-known book: poems about the boy
Grga Čvarak and the animals and people around him, long a grade-2 lektira
(required reading).

Told like a radio match report, until the last word.

The football words are old borrowings from English: *bek* (back, a defender),
*centarfor* (centre-forward), *gol* (goal); *krilo* is a wing, as in English.
*Sistem* is the team's formation. *Pucalo se preko krila*, "shooting went over
the wings", is the football phrase for play down the wings. A goal *pada* (falls)
in Croatian when it is scored; English has it "go in". *Žabac* is a male frog,
*žapci* the plural; *mušica*, a little fly, is a gnat or midge.

The poem rhymes its 2nd and 4th lines (*utakmica* / *mušica*); the rhymes are not
kept. The object of the last line comes first, so the swallowing is held back to
the end, as in the Croatian.

Text from the 4th edition of *Grga Čvarak* (Mladost, 1974), matching the full text
in Mirko Kuten's school anthology *Hrvatski dječji pisci* (1991), and the last
stanza as quoted in Ivo Zalar's *Suvremena hrvatska dječja poezija* (1979).
Lukin portal za djecu (pjesmicezadjecu.com) has "burnom" for "burnome".
