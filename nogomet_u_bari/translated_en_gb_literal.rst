####################
Football in the Pond
####################

| In the pond was played
| a football match
| the frogs played against
| the mosquitoes and midges.
|
| A new system was played
| shots came in from the wings
| and the whole match
| was at a stormy pace.
|
| But when the game was
| at the peak of sporting zeal,
| and when the mosquitoes had come
| on the attack up to the goalkeeper,
|
| the frog-back, in great fear
| that a goal might go in
| the opponents' centre-forward
| simply — swallowed.
