#############
Djedove obrve
#############

*English translation "Grandfather's Eyebrows".*

:Written by: Vesna Parun
:Date: circa 1971

Grandfather's eyebrows show every mood, until he falls asleep and they turn white as snow.

Notes
=====

Vesna Parun is one of the great Croatian poets of the 20th century, and wrote much
for children. This is free verse, with a rhyme here and there (*mene* /
*pozelene*, *moru* / *odgovoru*, *dolje* / *šolje*), which is not kept.

*Ko* is the spoken form of *kao* (like). *Čun* is a small boat. *Šta*, *dešava se*
and *šolja* (cup) are the eastern forms of *što*, *događa se* and *šalica*;
Parun lived for many years in Belgrade. *Tako, tako* is literally "so, so", said
as one says "well, well", which the translation uses. *Klonule* is "drooping",
worn out.

Text from Lukin portal za djecu (pjesmicezadjecu.com, "Bakama i djedovima"),
the only full copy found. The date is the first time the poem is found in print,
in a 1971 bibliography of periodicals (*Bibliografija Jugoslavije*).
