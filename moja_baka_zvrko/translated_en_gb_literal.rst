##############
My Grandmother
##############

| If you meet my grandmother
| in the street or in a lane
| go up to her with a light heart,
| as if she were your grandmother.
|
| Help her, little friend,
| for her legs serve her poorly,
| and her eyes see with difficulty too,
| so my grandmother walks slowly.
|
| Through the swarm of carts and cars
| lead her across the road,
| so that in the bustle of the noisy town
| my grandmother comes to no harm.
|
| You will recognise my grandmother
| by her walk, and by her step,
| and by her white, grey hair,
| and by the stick that she carries.
|
| And when grandmother comes home,
| her heart will beat happily,
| about you she will — all in happiness —
| tell me many lovely things.
