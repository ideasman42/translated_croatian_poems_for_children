#########
Moja baka
#########

*English translation "My Grandmother".*

:Written by: Ratko Zvrko
:Date: circa 1967

A child asks other children to help their grandmother across the busy road.

Notes
=====

From *Grga Čvarak* (Mladost, 1967), Zvrko's best-known book, long a grade-2 lektira
(required reading).

*Sokak* is a narrow lane, a word from Turkish; *il'* is *ili* (or). *Kola* is a cart,
and also a car; next to *auta* (cars) it is the older word. *Sijeda* is grey (of
hair); *bijela, sijeda kosa* is hair both white and grey.

The poem rhymes in couplets (*baku* / *sokaku*, *laka* / *baka*); the rhymes are not
kept.

Text from the 4th edition of *Grga Čvarak* (Mladost, 1974), p. 39, as a scanned copy
read by OCR, which has "ii'" for *il'* and a semicolon after *štapu*, taken to be
a comma. The date is that of the book's first edition; the poem is confirmed only in
the 1974 edition. Lukin portal za djecu (pjesmicezadjecu.com) has a different
version: "negdje samu na sokaku" (somewhere alone in a lane), "slabo ide" for
"sporo ide", and the 3rd and 4th stanzas the other way round.
