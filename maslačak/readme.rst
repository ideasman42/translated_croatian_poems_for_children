*English translation "The Dandelion".*

:Written by: Grigor Vitez

The dandelion has swapped its yellow gold coin for a lamp, to shine for the children who blow it.

Notes
=====

The yellow flower is the *dukat* (a gold coin, a ducat); the white seed-head that
follows it is the lamp, which children blow away.

*Trampa* is a swap or barter. *Kad me pušu* is literally "when they blow me";
"blow on me" is the natural English for blowing a dandelion. *Duša* is the soul,
here the children's.

The poem rhymes in couplets (*put* / *žut*, *trampu* / *lampu*); the rhymes are not
kept.

Text from *Grigor Vitez: Izabrane pjesme* (lektire.skole.hr), the edition the book
uses for Vitez, stanza breaks checked against the PDF page.

Quoted the same in Ivo Zalar's *Suvremena hrvatska dječja poezija* (1979) and
*Suvremena metodika nastave* (1985). No date of first printing was found.
