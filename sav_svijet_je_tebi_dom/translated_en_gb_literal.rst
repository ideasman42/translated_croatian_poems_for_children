##########################
All the World Is Your Home
##########################

| And rain and wind and snow
| and bora and lightning and thunder,
| everything seems lovely to you
| when you know you have your own home.
|
| Under the eaves the birds chirp.
| On the roof the stork calls.
| And the chimney throws off smoke
| into the eye of the blue [vault].
|
| In the window Mother sits.
| In the stove the fire coos.
| And outside the blizzard sweeps,
| bangs against the door, pounds…
|
| Oh, the world is sweet and dear
| seen in that light,
| and when you step over the threshold,
| all the world is your home.
