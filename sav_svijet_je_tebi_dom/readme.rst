######################
Sav svijet je tebi dom
######################

*English translation "All the World Is Your Home".*

:Written by: Enes Kišević

With a home to go back to, even a blizzard looks lovely, and the whole world becomes home.


Translation
===========

- ``translated_en_gb_literal.rst`` keeps as close to the Croatian as English allows
  for reading against the original.

It keeps line numbers matching the original.


Notes
=====

**The end of line 8 is reconstructed.** The only copy found online (Anto Zirdum's blog,
antozirdum.wordpress.com) breaks off at "u oko plavoga", with the stanza break lost.
*Svoda* - of the vault (of the sky) - is supplied in brackets: it completes the phrase and
rhymes with *roda*, as the other stanzas rhyme their 2nd and 4th lines.
Check it against a printed copy if one turns up.

*Bura* is the bora, the cold north-east wind of the Adriatic coast. It is kept as a name
rather than "gale", since a Croatian child would know it as that wind in particular.

*Guče* is the cooing of a dove or a contented baby; the fire in the stove "coos".

*Odbija* is to throw off or bounce back, as a wall does a ball; the chimney "throws off"
its smoke.

The last line returns the title: once you have a home, every place outside it is home too.
