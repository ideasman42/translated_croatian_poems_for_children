########################
How You Can Spare Mother
########################

| When you play and play,
| then put your toys away yourself,
| you are, in fact, sparing your mother.
|
| When you comb your hair yourself,
| wash your face yourself,
| you spare Mother's morning nerves.
|
| And when for school
| you pack your things yourself,
| you are really sparing your mother.
|
| When your shoes and trousers
| you keep from the mud,
| you save Mother two good hours.
|
| When your books and schoolbag
| you keep in order,
| you save Mother a whole wage.
|
| Only for Mother
| do not spare love,
| for giving love
| alone is worthwhile.
