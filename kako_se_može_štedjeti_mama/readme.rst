##########################
Kako se može štedjeti mama
##########################

*English translation "How You Can Spare Mother".*

:Written by: Enes Kišević

A child is shown all the small ways of sparing Mother - and the one thing never to spare.

Notes
=====

The poem turns on *štedjeti*: to spare, to save, to economise.
English needs two verbs for it: *štedjeti* is "spare" (sparing Mother, her nerves, love)
and *uštedjeti* - to save up an amount - is "save" (two hours, a wage).
The last stanza turns the word around: spare everything else, but never love.

"Plaću jednu" is literally "wage one", the number placed after the noun for the rhyme
with *redu*. It reads "a whole wage".
