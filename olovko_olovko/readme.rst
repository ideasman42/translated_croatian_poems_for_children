##############
Olovko, olovko
##############

*English translation "Pencil, Pencil".*

:Written by: Grigor Vitez

A child asks the pencil, the homework and the book to do the work themselves; playing he can do alone.

Notes
=====

The joke is in the reflexive verbs: *zašilji se*, *napiši se*, *nauči se* (sharpen
yourself, write yourself, learn yourself) ask the things to do the work; the ball
needs no help.

*Olovko*, *zadaćo*, *knjižice*, *lopto* are the vocative, the form used to call out
to someone; *knjižica* is the fond, small form of *knjiga* (book). *Zadaća* is
homework. *To ću sam* is "that I will (do) myself", the verb left out. *Igrati se
znam* puts the playing first, "to play, I know how"; the English keeps the plain
order.

Text from *Grigor Vitez: Izabrane pjesme* (lektire.skole.hr), the edition the book
uses for Vitez.

Also in *Izabrane pjesme i priče* (listed in the *Croatica* bibliography, 1973).
No date of first printing was found.
