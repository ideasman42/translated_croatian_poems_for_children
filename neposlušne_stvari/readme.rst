#################
Neposlušne stvari
#################

*English translation "Disobedient Things".*

:Written by: Grigor Vitez

A child can't find his socks, pencil or scarf, and his mother has a tall story for each.

Notes
=====

A child asks, and the mother answers with a story: the things have run off by
themselves, as the title says.

A *bijela vrana* (white crow) is the Croatian for something you will never see,
so the mother's first answer is a wink. *Nazuvati* is to pull on (socks or shoes).
*Šaren* is many-coloured or dappled.

The poem rhymes in couplets (*čarapama* / *mama*, *vranu* / *tavanu*); the rhymes
are not kept.

Text from *Grigor Vitez: Izabrane pjesme* (lektire.skole.hr); it is the title poem of a selection of Vitez's
poems (1980). Lukin portal za djecu (pjesmicezadjecu.com) sets the speech in
quotation marks, has "Olovčica" (little pencil) for "Olovka" and leaves out the dash
after "metnuo".
