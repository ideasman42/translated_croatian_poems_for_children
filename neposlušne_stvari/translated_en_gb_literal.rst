##################
Disobedient Things
##################

| – Oh, whatever has become of my socks?
| Have you seen them anywhere, mother?
|
| – Yes, I saw a white crow,
| Pulling them on this morning in the attic!
|
| – My pencil was here last night,
| And now it's gone. Wherever has it hidden?
|
| – Ah, yes, I remember:
| The tomcat to his late grandfather
| Was writing a letter,
| And where he put it –
| that we didn't see!
|
| – I left my scarf somewhere here,
| Tell me, mother, where would it be now?
|
| – This morning two colourful hares went by
| They say, they need it for the trip to the Moon!
