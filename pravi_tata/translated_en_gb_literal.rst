#############
A Real Father
#############

| A real father is
| a book full of the loveliest stories,
| the king of all kings
| for whom a washbasin serves — as a crown.
|
| A real father works wonders —
| he understands the languages of all animals,
| and often talks in them.
|
| A real father is a horse
| that all the kids in the street ride.
| He knows how to make cannons out of elder
| and out of chestnuts little pipes.
|
| A real father knows how to play at being a cow
| the sun, the sea and the grass.
| When needed he is a tree
| that the kids clamber up
| and in whose crown ripe
| kisses they pick.
