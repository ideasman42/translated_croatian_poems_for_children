##########
Pravi tata
##########

*English translation "A Real Father".*

:Written by: Zvonimir Balog
:Date: 1970

Everything a real father is: a storybook, a king, a horse, a cow, the sun, the sea, a tree full of kisses.

Notes
=====

"Kojemu lavor služi — kao kruna" is literally "to whom a washbasin serves - as a crown":
an upturned basin on his head. The English keeps that order, so the dash still marks
the pause before the punchline "as a crown".

*Bazga* is elder. Its hollow stems make a pop-gun, the "cannon" here;
a chestnut hollowed out with a twig for a stem makes a toy pipe.

*Klinci* is slang for kids. *Verati se* is to clamber.

*Igrati se krave* (with the genitive) is "to play cow", to pretend to be one,
on the pattern of *igrati se rata*, to play war.

*Krošnja* is the crown of a tree, so in English the word "crown" returns from the
first stanza. The echo is a chance of English only: the Croatian has two unrelated
words, *kruna* and *krošnja*.
