###########################################
Long into the Night, the White Winter Night
###########################################

| Long into the night, into the dead winter night
| My mother weaves white linen.
|
| Her bowed figure and her greying hair
| Have long since been drenched in tears.
|
| The lamp's beam from the window spans the whole yard
| Over the snow that falls outside
| In silence without end, in silence without end:
| Angels from heaven, with gentle hands,
| Let frozen little stars down to the earth
| Taking care not to wake my darling.
|
| Long into the night, into the empty winter night
| My mother weaves white linen.
|
| O, sorrowful mother! tell me, what is that shining
| In your eyes
|
| Long into the night, into the white winter night?
