###############################
Dugo u noć, u zimsku bijelu noć
###############################

*English translation "Long into the Night, the White Winter Night".*

:Written by: Dragutin Tadijanović
:Date: 1931

A son remembers his mother weaving late into a snowy winter night.

Notes
=====

Written for adults, not children, but long read in Croatian schools and on Mother's Day.
It is sadder than the rest of the set.

The refrain changes its word for the night each time: *gluha* (deaf - the dead of night),
*pusta* (empty, desolate), and at last *bijela* (white), the snow.

*Platno* is linen or homespun cloth, woven at home on a loom.

*Zlato moje* (my gold) is a common endearment, "my darling".

*Odavna je već zališe suzama* is an aorist with no stated subject; the translation
gives it as a passive, as Croatian readers take it: her figure and hair have long
since been drenched in tears.

The text follows *Vijenac* 348 (Matica hrvatska, 2007), where the poem is dated
"Rastušje, sredinom kolovoza 1931." (Rastušje, mid-August 1931).
Other printings, likely a later revision, differ in a few words (*pognuti*, a second
"bijelu noć" in place of "pustu noć", "O mati žalosna!").
