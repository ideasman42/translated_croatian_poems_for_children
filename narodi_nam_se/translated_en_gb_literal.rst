##########
Born to Us
##########

| Born to us is the heavenly King
| of Mary, the pure Virgin.
| In this young year we rejoice,
| to the young King we pray.
|
| After him follows Saint Stephen,
| the first martyr of the Lord God.
| In this young year we rejoice,
| to the young King we pray.
|
| May God give us health, and joy besides,
| in this young year plenty of everything!
| In this young year we rejoice,
| to the young King we pray.
