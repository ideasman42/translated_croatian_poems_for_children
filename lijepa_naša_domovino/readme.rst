*English translation "Our Beautiful Homeland".*

:Written by: Antun Mihanović
:Date: 1835

The Croatian national anthem, which children learn at school.

Notes
=====

The words are from Antun Mihanović's poem "Horvatska domovina" (1835); the anthem uses
four of its stanzas, set down in law in 1990. Out of copyright.

The language is 19th-century: *kano* (as), *vazda* (always), *djedovina* (the land of
one's forefathers), *hrašće* (oaks, collectively), *grobak* (a little grave).

The second stanza praises the country's two halves: the plains (*ravna*) of the north
and east, and the mountains (*planina*).

The Drava, Sava and Danube are its three great rivers; *sinje more* is the Adriatic.
*Bura* is the bora, the cold north-east wind of the coast.
