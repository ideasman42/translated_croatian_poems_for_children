######################
Our Beautiful Homeland
######################

| Our beautiful homeland,
| O heroic land so dear,
| Our forefathers' land of ancient glory,
| may you be happy for ever!
|
| Dear, as you are glorious to us,
| Dear, you are our only one.
| Dear, where you are flat,
| Dear, where you are mountain!
|
| Flow, Drava, Sava, flow,
| Nor you, Danube, lose your might,
| Grey-blue sea, tell the world,
| That the Croat loves his people.
|
| While the sun warms his fields,
| While the bora blows through his oaks,
| While the grave hides his dead,
| While his living heart beats!
