#########
Moja mama
#########

*English translation "My Mother".*

:Written by: Enes Kišević
:Date: circa 1980

A short poem about a mother, at the centre of everything, who turns only around her child.


Translation
===========

- ``translated_en_gb_literal.rst`` keeps as close to the Croatian as English allows
  for reading against the original.

It keeps line numbers matching the original.


Notes
=====

The poem turns on one verb, *okretati se*: to turn, to turn around, to revolve.
Father *turns* - he turns his head to look at Mother, struck by her beauty;
the Earth and the Sun *turn* - they revolve.
English "turn" covers both senses, so it is kept as the one verb throughout
rather than splitting it into "turns to look" and "revolves".

*Oko* is "around" in both senses too: Mother turns around the child as a planet goes around the sun.

"Zbog ljepote njene" is literally "because of beauty hers"; the possessive after the
noun is a poetic word order in Croatian. English has no equivalent, so it reads
"because of her beauty".

*Mama* and *Tata* are capitalised as names in the source; "Mother" and "Father" follow.

The text follows the version on Lukin portal za djecu (pjesmicezadjecu.com). Other printings
lower-case *mama*, end the second line with a full stop instead of a colon, or title the poem
*Moja majka* ("My Mother").
