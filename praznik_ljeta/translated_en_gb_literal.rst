#####################
The Holiday of Summer
#####################

| June spreads the splendour of summer.
| In front of the school the lime tree blooms.
|
| On all sides sway
| branches full of golden dust.
|
| Over the wheat the larks rise.
| The last days of school arrive.
|
| Goodbye blackboards, desks, books,
| goodbye heavy schoolchild cares!
|
| Now children all over the world
| will celebrate the holiday of blazing summer.
|
| And when the summer days have passed,
| wistful clouds will arrive.
|
| And when the branches are bare,
| full of sun will be – the schools...
