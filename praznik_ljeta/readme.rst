#############
Praznik ljeta
#############

*English translation "The Holiday of Summer".*

:Written by: Gustav Krklec
:Date: circa 1975

June, the lime tree in flower and the last days of school: goodbye to books until the autumn.

Notes
=====

The Croatian school year ends in June. *Lipanj* (June) is named after the *lipa*
(lime tree, linden), which flowers then; the *zlatni prah* (golden dust) is its
pollen. In the last couplet the summer's sun is carried back into school in autumn.

*Đačke brige* are a pupil's cares. *Slavit* is *slaviti* (to celebrate), shortened.
*Žarko ljeto* is blazing (scorching) summer, stronger than merely hot. *Praznik* is
both a festive day and a day off school; *holiday* keeps both senses.

The poem is in rhymed couplets (*ljeta* / *cvjeta*); the rhymes are not kept.

Text from Lukin portal za djecu (pjesmicezadjecu.com), matching Krklec's
*Majmun i naočari* (*Odabrana djela*, 1977) and Zvonimir Balog's anthology
*Zlatna knjiga svjetske poezije za djecu* (1975) as far as their snippets show;
the date is that of the 1975 anthology, the earliest printing found.
