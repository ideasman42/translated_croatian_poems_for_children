#########################
What Colour Is the Stream
#########################

| To the stream that flows through the wood
| Cautiously a stag came up
| And having drunk of the water said:
| – The stream is green like the wood.
|
| By the rocks the stream flows on
| Breaking against the ribs of stone,
| Hopping, a bunny said:
| – This stream is all of silver.
|
| Through the field the stream flows on
| Having come out under the clear sky,
| And a swallow from above said:
| – The stream is blue like the sky.
|
| And the stream flows on, flows on,
| Over it white clouds stand,
| Hovering, a white butterfly said:
| – This stream is white in colour.
|
| With a host of stars came the evening,
| The day put out its colours,
| A timid doe quietly said:
| – Look, the whole stream is full of stars.
