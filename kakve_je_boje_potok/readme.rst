###################
Kakve je boje potok
###################

*English translation "What Colour Is the Stream".*

:Written by: Grigor Vitez
:Date: 1959

A stag, a bunny, a swallow, a butterfly and a doe each say what colour the stream is, and each is right.

Notes
=====

The stream takes the colour of whatever is over it, and each animal sees it where
it lives. A favourite in Croatian school readers since the 1970s, for grades 2 and 3.

*Šumom* is "through the wood", and also sounds like *šum* (a murmur). *Ozvjezdan* is
a word of the poet's own, "starred", as a sky is. *Veče* is *večer* (evening).
*Dalje teče, teče* is given as "flows on, flows on"; the Croatian has "on"
(*dalje*) only once.

The poem rhymes its 1st and 3rd lines on *teče* / *reče* in every stanza; the rhymes
are not kept.

From *Kad bi drveće hodalo* (Mladost, 1959). Text from Vitez's own
*Kao lišće i trava* (1960), matching *Grigor Vitez: Izabrane pjesme*
(lektire.skole.hr) and Zvonimir Balog's anthology (1975). Some copies, among them
Mirko Kuten's anthology (1991) and an e-textbook for grade 5, wrongly repeat
"I napivši se vode reče:" at the start of the 4th stanza. The author's edition has
no question mark in the title.
