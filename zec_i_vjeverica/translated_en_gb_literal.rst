#########################
The Hare and the Squirrel
#########################

| To the squirrel the bob-tailed bunny
| the other day under a beech said:
| — If I had such a tail,
| like the sun I would be beautiful!
|
| The squirrel as if in jest
| says to the hare: — Don't be silly!
| Mind your own business,
| and don't touch my little tail.
|
| Everyone has something beautiful,
| which puts them ahead of others.
| Long ears are your gift,
| and a long tail — my affair!
