#############
Svraka i sova
#############

*English translation "The Magpie and the Owl".*

:Written by: Gustav Krklec
:Date: 1952

The magpie mocks the owl for living in the dark; the owl has the last word.

Notes
=====

From *Telegrafske basne* (Telegraph Fables, 1952): fables of four lines each, named
after the telegraph because Krklec wanted their messages short and quick.
The moral is left to the title and the last line, rather than spelled out.

The owl turns the magpie's own picture back on her: *svijetli* (bright) and
*rasvijetliti* (to light up) - the dark is in the magpie's head, not the owl's.

*A sova će* is literally "and the owl will", the verb of speaking left out,
as in a story told aloud.

Text from Lukin portal za djecu (pjesmicezadjecu.com), matching a transcription of
*Majmun i naočale* (Mladost, 1967) in čovjek-časopis; set in Profil Klett's school
reader (hr.izzi.digital).
