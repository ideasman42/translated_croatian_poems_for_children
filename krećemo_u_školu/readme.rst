###############
Krećemo u školu
###############

*English translation "We Are Off to School".*

:Written by: Josipa Franjić Radulović

The school bell rings at the start of the year, for the old pupils and the new first-years.


Translation
===========

Notes
=====

The first day of the school year, in September. A *prvašić* is a first-year pupil,
the fond, small form of *prvaš*; *đak* is a pupil.

*Al'* is *ali* (but). *Svi se njima sad ponose* can be read as everyone being proud
of the first-years, or the first-years being proud of their bags; "them" keeps both.

*Što ih čeka tko će znati* is "what awaits them, who will know"; the comma is added in
English so "them who" does not read as one phrase. *Školski sati* are the lessons
(a *sat* is a lesson period, literally an hour), not the hours school is open.

The poem rhymes in couplets (*zazvonilo* / *otvorilo*); the rhymes are not kept.

From the collection *Sreća* (Happiness), as published by Naklada BoMboN
(nakladabombon.hr), the same source as "Djeca u prometu". No other copy or date was
found.
