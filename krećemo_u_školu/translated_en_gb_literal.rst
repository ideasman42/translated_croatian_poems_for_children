####################
We Are Off to School
####################

| The school bell has rung
| has opened the doors of the school.
|
| Cheerfully now it calls the pupils
| the old ones, but the new ones too.
|
| The little first-years who are coming
| have filled the school paths.
|
| On their backs they carry bags
| all are proud of them now.
|
| What awaits them, who can know,
| the school lessons have begun.
